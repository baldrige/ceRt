An application asks a single Justice — normally the Circuit Justice for the court below — for relief while a case proceeds: a stay, an injunction, bail, or an order vacating one entered below. The Justice may rule alone or refer it to the full Court; a denial by one Justice can be refiled with another. Most applications are not this: about five in six ask only for more time to file, and are counted under All applications but not shown. Status: With the Justice, filed and not yet acted on; Response requested, the Justice has called for the other side’s response; Before the Court, referred to all nine. Administrative stay is a Justice’s temporary order holding the ruling below while the application is considered — not a ruling on it. Granted counts grants in part. The rate is of applications ruled on, leaving out those withdrawn. Applicant is read from the caption: United States is a federal party or officer, not the President sued in a personal capacity. Undecided in a closed Term means the docket was last read before its ruling. The Term is the docket number’s (25A### is OT2025). Read from the Court’s docket entries; an unrecognised ruling reads as pending — under 1% of the interim applications of OT2017–OT2023.
