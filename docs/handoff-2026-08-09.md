### The chain worth remembering

The feeds work turned up nothing. But the pending-docket cache surfaced two
"live" cases that weren't live, which surfaced two grammar gaps, and checking
*those* against the funnel surfaced a **published statistic that had been 2.5×
wrong for two weeks** — nobody was looking for it. Root cause: `funnel_baselines.json`
was generated 2026-07-10 and invalidated 2026-07-25 by the CVSG relist fix, with
nothing to notice. There is now a fingerprint (`funnel_baseline_fingerprint()`)
that digests the classifier's **function bodies**, not just its constants — the
July break was inside `classify_petition_events()`, so a constants-only guard
would have missed it.
