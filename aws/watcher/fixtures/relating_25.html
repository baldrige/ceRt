Opinions Relating to Orders - 2025


Opinions may be written by Justices to comment on the summary disposition of cases by orders, e.g., if a Justice wants to dissent from the denial of certiorari or concur in that denial. Any such opinions issued during October Term 2026 (October 5, 2026, through October 3, 2027), will be posted here, first in slip opinion format and later as edited to reflect the usual publication style of the United States Reports, including final pagination that will carry forward unchanged in the corresponding preliminary prints and the bound volumes of the United States Reports. For further information, see Table Definitions.
