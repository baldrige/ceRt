Daniel Grand v. City of University Heights, Ohio, et al.

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit, No. 24-3876 · judgment November 13, 2025

Certiorari granted · June 30, 2026
Pre-decision estimate: 4% cert probability

Before the decision, roughly the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for counsel who has won certiorari before, a Sixth Circuit decision below, and a petition filed soon after the judgment below, and down for a state or local-government respondent.

Question presented

Whether the First Amendment's established chilling-effect doctrine-under which a credible government threat that deters the exercise of fundamental rights constitutes a complete and independently actionable constitutional injury-is displaced by Williamson Cnty.'s land-use finality requirement when a plaintiff alleges that government threats both before and after a Planning Commission meeting chilled religious exercise, worship, and assembly.
