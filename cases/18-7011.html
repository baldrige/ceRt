John S. Myzer v. George W. Bush, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit, No. 18-3067 · judgment September 13, 2018

Certiorari denied · February 19, 2019

Question presented

May a district court dismiss a case with prejudice that has not begun? Should a delay of many years between the dismissal of a case and a Fed.R.Civ.P. 60(b)(4) Motion to set aside the dismissal effect its review? Does a party consent to a court's exercising personal jurisdiction over a case with parties that are not later served? Can a court lack personal jurisdiction over all defendants in a case and still exercise jurisdiction over the case? May a court deny a plaintiff access to service of process through law enforcement because he paid the filing fee in a case alleging fraud as to the name and address of the defendants? Was the District Court's dismissal with prejudice on the pleadings, pursuant to Fed.R.Civ.P. 8, 12(b)(6), and 41(b), a denial of due process under the facts? May a court dismiss a case with prejudice for lack of subject matter jurisdiction? Maya court decide other issues jVxw if it lacked subject matter jurisdiction? Did the Court improperly dismiss for lack of subject matter jurisdiction? Was the District Court's dismissal a final, appealable order? Did the lower courts deny the plaintiff due process when they failed to order a hearing to determine the Plaintiffs mental capacity, pursuant to Fed.R.Civ.P. 60(b)(6)?
