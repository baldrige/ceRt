Deon Lewis Duke v. Microsoft Corporation, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 22-2455 · judgment September 14, 2022

Certiorari denied · February 27, 2023

Questions presented

  1. Am I delusional? Am I irrational? Am I frivolous?

  2. Is this a normal reaction to gaslighting & gang stalking?

  3. Have you all been protecting hackers, con artists/frauds, human trafficking, criminals/corruption, all implementing these aforementioned methods with cohersion, fear, drugs, sex, guilt, death & incarceration as their so called “DEFEND TRADE SECRETS ACT of 2016”?

  4. Can the Big Data Act of 2016 be used for dissemination about my persons, property & organizations?

  5. Why are Charter lawyers, The CEO of Alphabet, and Facebook and Microsoft employees constantly stalking my social and business networking sites and in real life, if as testified in the lower courts “no appellees were served?”

  6. Who has been stealing my work and credit and property, sabotaging and manipulating my reality for profit at the expense of the country?

  7. Does this represent just a domestic threat or war crimes?

  8. Have technology companies created an addictive and narcotic like effect acting detrimentally, neglectfully or even maliciously against their subscribers?

  9. Can a 4th sovereign state arise out the ashes of the industrial age and the information age propel us to safely transfer our species to intergalactic colonization and obtain type 1 civilization?

  10. Is it time to redefine such concepts as currency, value, property, transport, state, commerce, highway, identity and location?

  11. Does the DTSA preempt existing state regulations?

  12. “Would you like to know more?” - ROBERT HEINLEIN copyright 1976
