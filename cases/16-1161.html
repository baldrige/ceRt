Beverly R. Gill, et al., Appellants v. William Whitford, et al.

Paid petition · United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, No. 15-cv-421-bbc · judgment November 21, 2016

Decided · June 18, 2018
Pre-decision estimate: 8% cert probability (95% interval 4%–18%)

Before the decision, about 2.1× the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for a direct appeal from a three-judge district court (mandatory jurisdiction).

Questions presented

1.Did the district court violate Vieth v. Jubelirer, 541 U.S. 267 (2004), when it held that it had the authority to entertain a statewide challenge to Wisconsin's redistricting plan, instead of requiring a district-by-district analysis?

  1. Did the district court violate Vieth when it held that Wisconsin's redistricting plan was an impermissible partisan gerrymander, even though it was undisputed that the plan complies with traditional redistricting principles?

  2. Did the district court violate Vieth by adopting a watered-down version of the partisangerrymandering test employed by the plurality in Davis v. Bandemer, 478 U.S. 109 (1986)?

  3. Are Defendants entitled, at a minimum, to present additional evidence showing that they would have prevailed under the district court's test, which the court announced only after the record had closed?

  4. Are partisan-gerrymandering claims justiciable? FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF THE QUESTION OF JURISDICTION IS POSTPONED TO THE HEARING OF THE CASE ON THE MERITS. JURISDICTION NOTED 6/19/2017
