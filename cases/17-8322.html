Lyle Roger Harrison v. Illinois

IFP petition · Appellate Court of Illinois, Fourth District, No. 4-17-0100 · judgment November 15, 2017

Certiorari denied · April 30, 2018

Question presented

The Peter Lux Jr. Estate of ±960 acres of prime farmland was settled in 1923. Moultrie County agreed to settle via Springing Executory Interest, the Peter Lux Jr. Estate on the 9 unborn Harrison Children. Judge John Laughlin with the Harrison family signed the Agreed Order in Moultrie Case 36-CH-63 stating, the Peter Lux Jr. Estate and Newton A. Trabue Estate, consisting of +1440 acres, is held for, the Harrison Children in Trust FOREVER. From 1973-2018(+44 years), Hardware State Bank and shareholder, Judge Dan Flannell, confiscated ±$23 Million of Trust income and ±$1 Million in (FSA)Federal farm Subsidies(Trust funds). The Illinois Appellate Court 4th District, and Illinois Supreme Court blessed this regime. The questions presented: Whether due process allows the prosecutor to use a criminal case in order to: absolve a million dollar liability, coerce and incarcerate Lyle until he signs his trust funds to Judge Dail L. Flannell or Judge Flannell’s Bank? Whether Lyle R. Harrison has the right to speedy trial?
