Rancourt Woodell v. Cynthia Link, Superintendent, State Correctional Institution at Graterford

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit, No. 17-2518 · judgment October 3, 2017

Certiorari denied · May 14, 2018

Question presented

WHETHER THE DISTRICT COURT’S FAILURE TO APPLY THE ONE-YEAR STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS UNDER SECTION 2244 (D)(1) TO ITS MAY 1 2 2006 10 NOVEMBER 14, 2006 CALCULATIONS TO OR FOR PETITIONER’S CASE, AS IT DID IN NARA-v-FRANK (488 F.3d 187 •(3rd Cir. 2007)), HUMMEL-v-ROSEMEYER (564 F.3d 290 (3rd Cir. 2009)), COMMONWEALTH-v-CRUZ (No. 852 A.2d 287, 288, 297 (Pa. 2004)), AND COMMONWEALTH-v-CHRUPALYK (No. 1686 EDA 2012 (April 4, 2013)) TOLLING ALL FILING DEADLINES DURING THE PERIODS OF MENTAL HEALTH ILLNESSES, DEFECTS, CONDITIONS AND DISORDERS, WAS AN ERROR OF LAW, REQUIRING REVERSAL AND REMAND FOR DEVELOPMENT OF A FACTUAL FINDING RECORD ON WHAT IMPACT PETITIONER’S SEVERE AND FREQUENT SEIZURES, DEFECTS, CONDITIONS AND DISORDERS, STEMMING FROM PETITIONER’S UNDIAGNOSED SERIOUS MENTAL HEALTH CONDITIONS, DEFECTS, DISORDERS AND ILLNESSES MAY HAVE HAD AT THE TIME OF THE OFFENSE, DURING INTERROGATION, WHILE IN CUSTODY, TRIAL, DIRECT APPEAL, PCRA AND FEDERAL HABEAS CORPUS PROCEEDINGS? WHETHER THE DISTRICT COURT FAILED TO CONDUCT AN EVIDENTIARY HEARING BASED UPON PETITIONER’S SERIOUS DIAGNOSED MEDICAL CONDITION OF SEVERE AND FREQUENT SEIZURES, STEMMING FROM HIS UNDIAGNOSED MENTAL HEALTH ILLNESSES, DEFECTS, DISORDERS AND CONDITIONS, RESULTING IN BRAIN SURGERY, RECOVERY TIME AND TREATMENTS WITH OTHER PYSCHOTROPIC AND SEIZURE MEDICATIONS AND TREATMENTS, WHICH PHYSICALLY HAMPERED HIS ABILITY TO DRAFT, DRAW, PREPARE AND SUBMIT A TIMELY PETITION WITHIN THE VERY STRICT TIME SAINTS OF THE AEDPA, WHEN NEUROLOGISTS AND OTHER TREATING MEDICAL AND MENTAL HEALTH PHYSICIANS, SURGEONS, SPECIALIST AND PERSONNEL HAVE REPEATEDLY OFFERED TO TESTIFY ABOUT PETITIONER’S MENTAL HEALTH AND MEDICAL CONDITION, WITH EACH REQUEST BEING SUBSEQUENTLY DENIED WITHOUT EXPLAINATION GIVEN? WHETHER THE DISTRICT COURT’S NOTICE OF ELECTION PROVIDING HIM WITH THE OPTION TO EITHER WITHDRAW HIS MAY 1, 2006 FILED HABEAS CORPUS 10 REF]1E AN ALL-INCLUSIVE HABEAS PETITION WITHIN A YEAR OF THE DISTRICT COURT’S ORDER GRANTING PERMISSION TO WAIVE HIS ALREADY FILED MAY 1 1 2006 HABEAS PETITION COULD MISLEAD A PRO SE PETITIONER, SUCH AS IRE PETITIONER, TO BELIEVE THAT HE HAD AN ADDITIONAL ONE-YEAR FILING DEADLINE TO REFILE AN ALL-INCLUSIVE HABEAS PETITION, AS THE DISTRICT COURT’S NOTICE OF ELECTION IMPLIES; OR TO PROCEED WITH THE MAY 1, 2006 FILED HABEAS CORPUS PETITION, WAIVING ANY FUTURE CLAIMS THAT MAY BE AVAILABLE? IF SO, DOES THE PRO SE PETITIONER’S CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHT TO CONFER WITH COUNSEL ATTACH SINCE THE DISTRICT COURT’S NOTICE OF ELECTION FORM IS ASKING A PRO SE PETITIONER, WHO IS UNSKILLED IN THE SCIENCE AND SKILL OF LAW, TO WAIVE RIGHTS THAT MAY BE AVAILABLE TO HIM AT A LATER DATE OR TO PROCEED WITH HIS PRO SE HABEAS PETITION “AS IS” WAIVING ANY POSSIBLE CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS THAT MAY BE PRESENTLY AVAILABLE TO HIM IN HIS PRESENT FILED PETITION THAT MAY HAVE BEEN MISTAKENLY OMITTED BY THE PRO SE PETITIONER, WHO IS UNSKILLED IN THE SCIENCE AND SKILL OF LAW? WHETHER THE DISTRICT COURT’S RULING THAT 196 DAYS OF PETITIONER’ S ONE-YEAR FILING LIMIT HAD ELAPSED, IN SPITE OF PETITIONER BEING REPRESENTED BY APPOINTED COUNSEL FROM THE TIME OF PETITIONER’S ARREST THROUGHOUT JULY 18, 2012 THE TIME PERIOD IN QUESTION WITH RESPECT TO THE TIME PERIOD -MAY 1 1 2006 THROUGH NOVEMBER 14, 2006- AND NOVEMBER 14, 2006 THROUGH JULY 18, 2012 THE DENIAL OF THE PENNSYLVANIA SUPREME COURT’S DENIAL OF ALLOCATUR HAD EXPIRED (MEMORANDUM, pp. 7-9, filed June 26, 2017), BEFORE RESUMING NOVEMBER 14, 2006?
