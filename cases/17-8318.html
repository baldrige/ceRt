Cordell Felix v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, No. 16-16457-DD · judgment November 8, 2017

Certiorari denied · April 30, 2018

Questions presented

In Terry v. Ohio, this Court held that the Fourth Amendment permits a police officer to stop and briefly detain a person “for purposes of investigating possibly criminal behavior even though there is no probable cause to make an arrest,” as long as there is reasonable articulable suspicion that the individual has committed or is about to commit a crime. 392 U.S. 1, 22 (1968). However, a “reasonable suspicion” requires more than a mere hunch: it requires an objectively reasonable justification, viewed in the totality of the circumstances. United States v. Sokolow, 490 U.S. 1, 7 (1989). And in United States v. Hensley, this Court indicated that a higher threshold may be required for reasonable suspicion following a completed crime, but the Court has yet to provide guidance about that distinction. 469 U.S. 221 (1985). This case involves a Terry stop wherein law enforcement was searching for two robbery suspects based on the general description of “two young black men on foot wearing dark clothing,” and stopped Petitioner, a young black man wearing a dark shirt a half mile from the reported crime. The questions presented are:

  1. Whether the general description, “two young black men on foot wearing dark clothing,” is too vague to support a finding of reasonable suspicion to Terry stop any young black man wearing a dark shirt, in an area that is at least half African American?

  2. Does a court err by relying on an individual’s presence in a high crime area to support a finding of reasonable suspicion where the crime has occurred and law enforcement is searching for suspects that match a given description?

  3. Whether 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(1) is facially unconstitutional because it exceeds Congress’s authority under the Commerce Clause, and is unconstitutional as applied to the intrastate possession of a firearm and ammunition?
