Wilmar Rene Duran-Gomez v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 20-20147 · judgment December 23, 2020

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021

Question presented

Whether, under the balancing test in Barker v. Wingo, 407 U.S. 514 (1972), prosecutors may prevent attachment of a defendant’s Sixth Amendment speedytrial right by effecting a pretextual arrest for an unre­ lated civil immigration offense and delaying indictment while they continue their criminal investigation.
