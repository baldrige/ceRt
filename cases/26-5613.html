Cory Sullivan v. Jessi Skelton

IFP petition · Supreme Court of Pennsylvania, Middle District, No. 30 MM 2026 · judgment February 17, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

A. WHETHER THE GOVERNMENT PURSUANT TO THE COMMONWEALTHS RULES OF APPELLATE PROCEDURE, SPECIFICALLY RULES OF PROCEDURE INVOLVING CHILD CUSTODY MATTERS, VIOLATE DUE PROCESS UNDER THE FOURTEENTH AMENDMENT OF THE UNITED STATES CONSTITUTION? SUGGESTED ANSWER: YES B. SHOULD THIS COURT GRANT THE WRIT WITH RESPECT TO THE LOWER COURTS CONTINOUS DENIAL OF PETITIONER'S DUE PROCESS CLAIMS BECAUSE:

  1. THE CHANGING OF PROCEDURE WITHIN THE UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE, RELATING TO HOW LEGAL MAIL IS NOW PROCESSED AND DATED IS CAUSE FOR REVIEW;

  2. THE COMMONWEALTH'S RULING IN THIS CASE, THE EQUIVALENCY OF INVOLUNTARILY TERMINATING A FATHER'S PARENTAL RIGHTS, WERE CONTRARY TO CLEARLY ESTABLISHED STATE AND FEDERAL LAW;

  3. THERE ARE EVIDENTIARY ERRORS THAT AMOUNTED TO A DEPRIVATION OF DUE PROCESS, AND THE HOLDINGS OF THE LOWER COURTS CONFLICT WITH PROCEDURE AND SEVERAL DECISIONS OF STATE AND FEDERAL COURTS? SUGGESTED ANSWER: YES
