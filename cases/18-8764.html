Tatyana Evgenievna Drevaleva v. Department of Veterans Affairs, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 18-17307 · judgment January 24, 2019

Certiorari denied · June 3, 2019

Question presented

: Shall the District Court assign an Attorney to a Title VII Plaintiff at the Plaintiff's request as outlined at 42 U.S.C. §2000e-5(O(1)? Shall the Title VII Plaintiff initially be looking for the Attorney herself at her own expense before asking the Court to assign the Attorney as outlined at 42 U.S.C. §2000e-5(f)(1)? Shall the Court assign an Attorney to a Title VII Plaintiff only after the Plaintiff demonstrates that she was unable to get the Attorney at her own expense? Does the Title VII Plaintiff have a right for an interlocutory Appeal of the District Court's Order under 28 U.S.C. §1291 and 28 U.C.S. §1292 as outlined at 42 U.S.C. §2000e-50)? Page 2 of 24 II.
