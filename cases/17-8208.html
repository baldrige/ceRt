Melissa L. Burnett v. BAC Home Loans Servicing, LP, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 17-2018 · judgment October 6, 2017

Certiorari denied · May 29, 2018

Question presented

Whether Officers of the Court can be allowed to commit bankruptcy fraud in U.S. Bankruptcy Court in violation of 18 USC 157 in order to discriminately deny U.S. citizens, including petitioner, of their Fifth Amendment Constitutional right to due process after voting for a Republican President in 2000 and 2004 due to Christian beliefs, which would also violate U.S. citizens, including petitioner's First Amendment Constitutional right to Free exercise of religion? Whether Officers of the Court can be allowed to commit fraud upon the court to cover up their politically motivated discrimination to cause intentional loss of property to U.S. citizens in bankruptcy court, including petitioner? Whether the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Illinois erred by mistaking Rule 60(b)(3) for Rule 60(d)(3), which has no time limitation to set aside a judgment for fraud upon the court? Whether petitioner is allowed to timely file her appeal (within 60 days) under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Federal Circuit Court of Appeals, due to the U.S. Chapter 13 Trustee and his Attorney being employees of the U.S. Government and named defendants, instead of being Ordered by a prejudiced U.S. Seventh Circuit Court of Appeals to file with the U.S. Seventh Circuit Court of Appeals that violates 28 USC 455(a) mandatory recusal due to prior personal knowledge of disputed evidentiary facts concerning case?
