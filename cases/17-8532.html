A REVIEW TO ESTABLISH A CAP ON THE DAMA GES. Subject PUBLISHED Opinion allows arbitrary damages WITHOUT ANY CAP even a'ainst Strangers EVENA GAINS T WINNING PARTY IN OTHER CASE. Therefore, We have the following issues for the review: Should an extra-ordinarily illegal Judgment obtained without even informing defendants having punitive damages based on the GROSS assets of Strangers but falsely claimed by plaintiff belonging to defendants be allowed? Should plaintiff be allowed for the extra-ordinarily illegal judgment from a different court [Superior Court] when plaintiff did not appeal the Unlawful Detainer Court judgment of eviction in favor of Defendant Singh? As Defendant Rawat NEVER saw Fernandes and NEVER communicated with Fernandes, Fernandes did not and can NEVER testify and/or even can NEVER plead against Rawat.
