CODE ANN. 99-39-21 WAS UNREASONABLY USED THE BAR TO PETITIONER’S FUNDAMENTAL ISSUES OF SEVERE IMPORTANCE THAT WHICH REVOLVES AROUND FEDERAL TREATIES WHETHER THE FUNDAMENTAL ISSUES PRESENTED BY THE PETITIONER SHOULD HAVE AT MINIMUM WARRANTED A RESONABLE CONSIDERATION / WHETHER MISS. CODE ANN. 99-39-21 SERVES AS AN ABSOLUTE BAR TO A PETITIONER’S FEDERAL ISSUES THAT CLEARLY REST UPON THE UNITED STATES CONSTITUTION’S GUARANTEES \ A.   1. Laqunn Gary' w'as convicted, follow'ing a trial, in the Circuit Court of Hinds County, Mississippi, before Honorable Jeff Weill.
