Andres Cabezas v. Richard Corcoran, Florida Commissioner of Education

IFP petition · District Court of Appeal of Florida, First District, No. 1D19-881 · judgment April 7, 2020

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021

Question presented

Did the reviewing court of a state agencyfs determination to revoke property overstep its judicial authority in not granting the parties' desire to remand the case to correct procedural error? Should a due process violation be presumed when a state agency fails to adhere to its established procedure in a property revocation proceeding?
