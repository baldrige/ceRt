Questions presented

  1. What transactions between an ERISA plan and a service provider are prohibited by 29 U.S.C. § 1106(a)(1)(C) unless exempt under 29 U.S.C. § 1108(b)(2)?

  2. Does a district court abuse its discretion when, for reasons other than futility, it denies plaintiffs an opportunity to amend their first complaint after granting a motion to dismiss under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(b)(6)?
