Question presented

In Monsanto Co. v. Durnell, 2026 WL 1825691 (U.S. June 25, 2026), this Court held that the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act “expressly preempts” “state-law failure-to-warn claim[s]” against Monsanto for failing to include “a cancer warning on Roundup’s label” when the Environmental Protection Agency has declined to require one. Id. at *13. In this case, the California Court of Appeal affirmed judgment on a state-law failure-to-warn claim brought against Monsanto for failing to warn consumers that its Roundup products allegedly cause cancer. The question presented is: Whether, in light of Durnell, this Court should vacate the decision of the California Court of Appeal and remand this case for further proceedings not inconsistent with Durnell.
