Paul Charles Sorensen v. Washington

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Washington, Division 1, No. 76329-6-I · judgment April 4, 2017

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

Can a DOC Corrections Records Manager send an e-mail to a prosecutor and legally request that a prisoner’s maximum term of his sentence be changed to “Life?” Can a prosecutor and a judge hold an open court action against said prisoner and issue an ORDER TO AMEND THE JUDGMENT AND SENTENCE to amend said prisoner’s maximum term to “Life” without the prisoner’s knowledge or legal representation in that open court action??[Ref APPENDIX “N”] Can a judge, in issuing said ORDER, ignore the fact that said prisoner’s J&S contract dismissed all aggravating circumstances against said prisoner, change said prisoners maximum term sentence to “Life” while ignoring that in the State of Washington a judge may nOt sentence a defendant/prisoner to “Life” without a jury’s findings that said defendant/prisoner was guilty of aggravating circumstances, una tmbisland by special interrogatory. Can a judge in issuing said ORDER, also ignore that in the State of Washington’s RCW 9.94a.507(i)”statute, the requirement by that statute that a sentence may be given a defendant/ prisoner only in the Standard Range specified for any criminal act and the sentence of “Life” can only be given after a jury’s finding of a defendant/prisoner guilty of aggravating circum-. stances in the criminal act charged? (-See ft 43(a)) In an open court action in which the prisoner has no knowledge or legal representation, can the court/judge have any off the street licensed attorney sign said ORDER as “attorneyfor-the--defendant” even though that attorney is not prisoner’s “attorney of record” and the prisoner had no knowledge of that attorney’s actions and had no knowledge of the said open court action? [Ref CrR 11(a) -FRAUD] -1- Does a prisoner need not be notified or otherwise informed of the said open court action against him nor be provided with a copy of any ORDER after said court’s issuance of one affecting said prisoner? Can said (illegal/fraudulent) ORDER be used to illegally alter prisoner’s original J&S contract to add “Life” as a maximum term, without said prisoner’s knowledge or a jury’s findings of guilt of aggravating circumstances, even though prisoner’s maximum term noted on his J&S contract was “inadvertently” left blank by the’ sentencing judge? Even though said alteration to add the illegal maximum term to the said prisoner’s J&S contract proves that his original J&S contract was not fully disclosed at the time of signing and, therefore, under the OBjective Theory of Contract which all law must abide,said prisoner’s J&S contract is already unconscionable and an act of fraud by contract law and may be nullified, rescinded and revoked back to it’s inception date. And it matters not that a contract in conflict with statutory requirements is illegal and considered unenforceable, as a matter of law, nor that a contract void under the Statute of Fraud is not subject to reformation, since the rights to reformation presupposes a valid contract. Even where a contract is within an agency’s substantive authority, failure to comply with statutory mandated procedure .is ultr vires and renders the contract void. Question then is, the State of Washington subject to those laws and the Justices of the WA State Supreme Court boundby them? With an original null and void unconscionable J&S contract and for sure a VOID J&S contract after it being illegally altered, as noted above, is said prisoner being held against his will or incarcerated by the State (DOC), technically, as a kidnap person/victim as defined by State of Washington’stature RCW 9A.40.030? - ii - In an open court, standing at the bar before the sitting judge’s bench, with the judge listening, is it okay for a prosecutor to change the criminal act element initially charged a defendant to something else read out of a lawbook, after the prosecutor standing in front of the sitting judge, agrees or otherwise admits to the defendant that his, the prosecutor’s original charge element of the crime charged was bogus, thereby, committing Fraud on the Court? Can the judge, after listening to the prosecutor admit his original charge is bogus and look up in a lawbook another element of a criminal act that would result in the same charge Count, accept the Petitioner’s guilty plea, knowing full well that there was no credible evidence to even establish a crime charged had even or ever happened, let alone Petitioner was guilty of committing it, regardles of what the Corpus Delict Rule of Law says? - iii -
