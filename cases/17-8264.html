Carston Carrell Woodson v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, No. 17-1818 · judgment January 8, 2018

Certiorari denied · May 21, 2018

Question presented

Whether, When, and to What implied limited authority constituted to adjudicators gives expansive discretion to act as a neutral arbiter that actively participates at the pleading stage, Shows forthwith appearance of partialities undermining the traditional adversarial relationship, to impinge constitutional protected rights to render a sua sponte dismissal of a pro se litigant civil complaint and without redetermination of indigency, When there has been no legitimate change to the prescribed poverty designation, Furthered by threatened thought control should it not support the particular legal theory he advances, Should impose a sanctioned invalidation upon the plaintiffs in forma pauperis ascribed status, Illuminates a perceivable impropriety to abridge the Freedoms of expression holding sufficient is an inalienable human right necessitated on a prescriptive constitutional floor, without giving the opportunity to present the merits of a claim of right protected interest violated by a defined conduct to bring a cause of action in that public forum, in which the person affected by When the Order does not have a full opportunity to assert property interests injured to raise a right to relief, given to ethical reasoning's that Whether it is inappropriate for the trial court de novo determination to raise an objection to a complaint and to dispose it thereof, constitute the infringement of constitutional rights afforded.
