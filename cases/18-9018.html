Jupiter Dennell Wilson, Sr. v. City of Chesapeake, Virginia

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, No. 18-1462 · judgment September 17, 2018

Certiorari denied · October 7, 2019

Question presented

Whether the United States Supreme Court with its judicial discretion is able to affirm the Fourth Circuit's Certification of this consolidated case("No.: 18-1462") after the United States Supreme Court has acquired sufficient knowledge of Fraud(andlor Error as related to Fraud) on the Record of the Courts as clearly explained and shown by the Appellant's Reasons for Granting the Writ? Whether the United States Supreme Court with its judicial discretion is able to grant Certiorari based on Appellant's assertions of the United States court of appeals' affirmation of the District Court's "Judgment in Civil Case(Doc. # 66, of consolidated case number 2:16-cv-00- 30 1), Opinion and Order(Doc. # 65), and Order(Doc 64)," of which is in conflict with the decision of another United States court of appeals on the same important matters(race and age discrimination and race and age retaliation)? Whether within this appeal of this consolidated case("No.: 18-1462") of which is not an appeal of right can the United States Supreme Court with its judicial discretion be able to determine that Appellant as it appears was assisted by the Court ill-judicially while causing to be subliminally subjected to Appellee's subjective defense that was camouflaged "as a scheme to interfere with the judicial machinery performing the task of impartial adjudication, as by preventing the opposing party(Appellant) from fairly presenting Appellant's case or defense," thus also inducing affirmation of the District Court's decision affirmed by the Fourth Circuit Court of Appeals to be in conflict with a decision of another United States court of appeals on the same important matters(race and age discrimination and race and age retaliation)?
