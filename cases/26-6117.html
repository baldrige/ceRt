In Re Hugo Cesar Granados

IFP petition

Pending

Questions presented

  1. If the Solicitor General of the United States is tasked with defending the United States before the Supreme Court, is he allowed to represent individual US employees and government agencies accused of’criminal conduct?

  2. If the Solicitor General is tasked with representing the United States, as required by 26 USC §7214, his duty and obligation would be to join a mandamus that enjoins or compels the criminal element within the United States to cease with their criminal acts, is this correct?

  3. Under what legal authority to disregard, ignore, and participate in criminal activity does the government agencies and government employees, the subject of this petition have?

  4. Why would the government agencies mandated and tasked by law with the protection of the United States and its citizens as well as the enforcement of the laws, allow the Internal Revenue Service to violate the laws and the Constitution, as well as causing irreparable harm and injury to thousands of individuals?

  5. If corruption is an official’s act, carried out with the intent . of giving advantage inconsistent with an official’s duties or the rights of others, would the actions of the government agencies listed in this petition meet the definition of corruption?

  6. If corrupt means having unlawful or depraved motives, do the actions of the government agencies listed in this petition fullfil this definition?

  7. If Supreme Court precedents have a direct application to most if not all of the cases presented in a district court, and pursuant to precedent, must be followed (precedent) by the lower courts, why!are they permitted to defy and disobey the Supreme Court?

  8. Does delaying the adjudication of a legal action, such as a habeas corpus petition or a claim of conspiracy by the government constitute facilitation and aiding and abetting?
