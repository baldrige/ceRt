In Re Roosevelt JeanLouis

IFP petition

Certiorari denied · March 26, 2018

Question presented

Whether the Petitioner has been denied the right to due process by the State Court not granting the same relief as others that are similarly situated under the same set of facts as applied to the law? Whether the Petitioner has been rightfully denied relief on jury instructions that have been found to constitute reversible error which others have obtained relief from? EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES WARRANTING REVIEW The Petitioner, Jeanlouis Roosevelt avers that this Court’s review is needed as he has filed this claim in the State Court and that Court has denied him the same relief as others that are similarly situated and that in and of itself, should warrant review before this Court. Discretionary review cannot be obtained in the State of Florida as the silent per curiam affirmed opinion of the State Appellate Court is not a decision that the State Supreme Court will accept jurisdiction on. Therefore, the Petitioner has attempted to file and seek relief with due diligence in the State Court but to no avail. The decision of this Court, were it to accept jurisdiction, would control on similar sets of facts when applying the same law to those facts, an essential quality to establish continuity in the Court’s decisions. The Appellant cannot make the claim presentable to the United States District Court or the Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals as that would require filing a §2244 and he does not have a claim of newly discovered evidence that is required to present the claim to them before presenting the claim to this court. Additionally, the Petitioner has already filed a 28 U.S.C. §2254 and according to the rules, he is not entitled to file an additional petition in the District court.
