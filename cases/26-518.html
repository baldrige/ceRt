Questions presented

  1. Whether a President’s Article II responsibilities warrant equitable tolling of his private civil claims while in office.

  2. Whether a federal court may invoke its inherent authority to impose monetary sanctions on the President and his lawyers based on their advocacy of a substantial, unresolved argument that Article II responsibilities warrant equitable tolling of the President’s private civil claims during his term.
