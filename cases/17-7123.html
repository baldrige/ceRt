The issue is whether the appellate court erred in upholding/affirming Alabama law pertaining to contracts when established law pertaining to contracts indicates that the language in the contract governs the contract. Moreover, “what is contained in the four walls of the contract” drives the contract. Alabama Courts have held time and again and it is settled Alabama law that they will not revise/modify or adjust contracts between parties; West End Say. Bank v. Goodwin 223Ala 185, 13580. 161 (1931); Me Donald V. US. Die casting and Development Co 585 so 2d 853 (Ala. 1991) Kahn v Terry 80. 2d 390 Ala. (1993); Fell v Witter Group Inc. 784 so. 2d 302, Ala. Civ. App (2000), Atlas Assurance Co. v. Byrne 235 Ala 281 178 so. 451 (1938). In this case the appellant court has seen fit to affirm the creation/addition of a provision to a contract when no such contract exist. Even if a written contract exist between parties and there is none, then the rights and duties are governed by the agreement Kthn v. Terry so. 2d 390 Ala. (1993). Those duties and rights specifically spell out that a “signed” supplemental agreement has to be present in order to activate the agreement.
