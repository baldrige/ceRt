Question presented

The executive branch arrested, detained, and subjected Petitioner, a lawful permanent resident, to removal proceedings based on his constitutionally protected speech and for the purpose of broadly chilling speech the executive disfavors. The questions presented are: (a) whether the Immigration and Nationality Act deprives federal courts of jurisdiction in habeas corpus to review the executive’s actions to punish and retaliate against a noncitizen through detention and removal proceedings, in circumstances where the administrative petition-for-review process could not offer meaningful review of “now or never” claims brought under the First and Fifth Amendments, and if so (b) whether that deprivation of habeas jurisdiction violates the Constitution as applied to Petitioner.
