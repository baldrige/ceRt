Robert Jones v. The Lamar Company, LLC, a Louisiana Limited Liability Company, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, No. 24-12949 · judgment March 2, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Whether the Rooker-Feldman doctrine bars a Federal District Court from exercising Subject-Matter Jurisdiction over an Independent Federal Complaint seeking damages for Fraudulent Property Liens under state law, where the alleged injury stems from the Defendants' Out-of-Court Misconduct rather than the State Court Three Judgments themselves, thereby conflicting with Skinner v. Switzer 562 U.S. 521, 531 (2011) App. B, also Exxon Mobil Corp. v. Saudi Basic Indus. Corp., 544 U.S. 280,284 (2005), and rulings from the Fifth and Sixth Circuits. Explanation: This question addresses the widening split among the Circuit Courts regarding the scope ofRooker-Feldman. It asks the Court to clarify whether Federal jurisdiction is stripped when a plaintiffchallenges an independent injury (the Fraudulent Liens under Fla. Stat. § 817.535) rather than asking the Federal Court to undo a state Court order directly. It highlights that a judgment issued without notice or hearing cannot bar a subsequent Federal suit on jurisdictional grounds.

  2. Whether a Federal Court sitting in Diversity under 28 U.S.C. § 1652 may expand state litigation privileges and judicial notice rules in a novel manner to dismiss an independent statutory claim with prejudice, where the underlying actions involved entirely different causes of action and distinct injuries. The Lower Court's decision conflicts with Supreme Court rulings, precedents of this Circuit, or other Circuits, warranting review by this Court to maintain consistency in this Court's rulings—notably, the Supreme Court case Erie Railroad Co. v. Tompkins, 304 U.S. 64 (1938). Explanation: This question targets the lower Court's failure to apply Florida substantive law properly. Itfocuses on the legal distinction between the 2017/2018 Eviction proceedings and the 2023 Fraudulent Lien action.
