Questions presented

This case returns to the Court. Previously, this Court granted review and vacated the judgment below that had held Respondent, Charleston County Sheriff’s Office (“CCSO”) —a county office for whose judgments the State of South Carolina bears no legal liability— was entitled to Eleventh Amendment immunity. This Court remanded for reconsideration of that immunity determination in light of Galette v. New Jersey Transit Corp., 607 U.S. 509 (2026). On remand, in a one-paragraph discussion, the Fourth Circuit reinstated its prior opinion and again held that CCSO remains entitled to the State’s Eleventh Amendment immunity: “Galette addressed the implications of the corporate form for the Eleventh Amendment analysis” and therefore “does not . . . directly bear on this case.” Pet. App. 4a–5a. Yet Galette expressly states: “The corporate form, however, is not the only structure that signals the State has created a legally separate entity.” 607 U.S. at 525. The questions presented are:

  1. Whether Galette is limited to only entities organized in corporate form.

  2. Whether the Charleston County Sheriff’s Office is an arm of the State of South Carolina.
