Jose Hernandez v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 19-50258, 19-50259 · judgment December 15, 2020

GVR'd · June 27, 2022

Question presented

Whether an attempted Hobbs Act robbery (18 U.S.C. § 1951) qualifies as a categorical “crime of violence” for purposes of 18 U.S.C. § 924(c)(3) because it “has as an element the use, attempted use, or threatened use of physical force against the person or property of another.” Id. - Prefix -
