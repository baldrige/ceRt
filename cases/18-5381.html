Question presented

Why has the Federal Reserve still not been asked to open its books to find out who exactly they have been giving backdoor help since 2001 that lead to '08 financial mortgage crises and which they have continued doing since '08, in spite of movies like "Inside Job", "Big Short", numerous attempts by Congress, media etc. requesting them to open their books and increasing evidence for the need for the same, as submitted by the defendant, who was the chief architect at the Federal Reserve in '07- '08? Why have EEOC, US Attorney General and other lawyers for defendants involved in this case requested proof that I was meeting or exceeding job description requirements for which I was hired for at these companies (submitted as evidence); and have done nothing once that proof was provided? Why have the lawyers for the named defendants basically done a cut & paste from previous cases they had worked on to show that my case has no merit (Between the lawyers for the 4 defendants 108 references were cited as reasons for dismissal, with computer search from no two law firms citing the same cases) and not been disciplined for the same? Since we in IT have developed the systems they use, we understand their limitations also very well. There was absolutely no connection between their references and the uniqueness of the 3 issues presented in my case. Why the collapse in areas like HR, Finance, Legal & IT across nearly every company and sector compared to the '90s with people unable to do the basic tasks of their own job descriptions, or the job descriptions they create for people reporting to them? Why no investigation into the World Bank Whistle Blower article claiming the existence of a mafia like cartel of central bank leaded headed by certain powerful individuals at the Federal Reserve and the connection to their using the 'easy money" they print, to place and promote their own people within these functions in some of the world's most powerful organizations? Why the increasing number of bullying, terrorism, accidents and strange incidents to my wife and others in my family since a similar writ of certeriori was filed in the US Supreme Court back in 2012? Why no action from the lower courts, in spite of the evidence submitted for the back/neck injuries done to my wife during the '08 NJ-VA relocation with the Federal Reserve which led to her disability; and which has continued with incidents like black mold in our home, wife's twin sister and father passing away in USA, my uncle's in India, Middle East and cousin in UK passing away under similar strange circumstances? Isn't it strange that our pet dog suddenly passed away on the same day that the US District Appeals Court passed a verdict against me on May 29, 2018? Why the continued indifference to the accuracy of my '08+ predictions on overprinting of money and increasing weather fluctuations/natural disasters, as well as the solutions that I have provided and implemented to reverse this per US President mandates, from my now 9/11 patent pending work?
