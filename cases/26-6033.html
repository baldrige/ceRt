Roman Vladimirovich Serpik v. Oklahoma

IFP petition · Court of Criminal Appeals of Oklahoma, No. MA-2026-673 · judgment September 23, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Whether Article IV, Section 4 of the United States Constitution is implicated where a state court refuses review of a constitutional challenge to Rule 8 enforcement, while related state proceedings are terminated by orders directing that future filings be returned unfiled, effectively foreclosing further state-court review of objections concerning court identity records, caption designations, notice, and judicial process.

  2. Whether due process under the Fourteenth Amendment is violated when a state court issues a court-fee bill, payment receipt, and Rule 8 enforcement order under the entity caption "ROMAN SERPIK"-LLC (DBA "ROMAN VLADIMIROVICH SERPIK"), despite the verdict identifying the defendant solely as "Roman Vladimirovich Serpik," a/k/a Roman Serpik, a/k/a Roman V. Serpik, and despite a binding appellate mandate (Okla. Sup. Ct. No. M-2023-910, Footnote 1) requiring inclusion of all versions of the name.

  3. Whether a Rule 8 payment order is constitutionally valid when the underlying fee bill contains billing errors, charges assessed to the LLC entity "ROMAN SERPIK," and was never corrected to reflect the verdict identity "Roman Vladimirovich Serpik," thereby preventing Petitioner from receiving lawful notice, a correct receipt, or meaningful opportunity to comply before enforcement.

  4. Whether due process is violated when the District Attorney charged the LLC entity "ROMAN SERPIK," mailed all notices to the business address of Roman Serpik LLC (105 Janets Way, Ste. C-228), and never corrected the caption to match the warrantless-arrest identity "SERPIK, ROMAN VLADIMIROVICH," resulting in mis-delivery, improper notice, structural identity variance, and a defective appellate caption ("Roman Serpik v. Oklahoma").
