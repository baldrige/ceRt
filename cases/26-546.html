Jon Myers v. David Nathan Myers, et al.

Paid petition · Appeals Court of Massachusetts, No. 25-P-785 · judgment February 12, 2026

Questions presented

  1. Does truth exist or matter anymore? And are our legal systems concerned with the threatened collapse of principle, values and accuracy?

  2. Is the Tort for the Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress, indeed a vital tool for American society and necessary in guiding today’s society? Within the context of elevating the Tort three parameters emerge: A) The gravity, irrationality and duration of the emotional distress inflicted on Petitioner Jon Myers is extraordinary, crossing state lines of Massachusetts, Pennsylvania and California. B) How understanding emotional abuse is so challenging and yet vital for Courts to have this deeper layer of psychological insight and C) That understanding the psychology of emotional abuse and emotional distress is crucial to understanding the health and well-being of these very United States.

  3. Could Jon Myers’ journey in the composite, setting aside in this Writ, an obsession with legal perfection, elevate the Tort and provide an outstanding opportunity to redirect for the better the psychology of the United States of America?
