Hamed Kian v. Florida

IFP petition · District Court of Appeal of Florida, Fourth District, No. 4D2024-2370 · judgment October 16, 2025

Certiorari granted · June 15, 2026

Question presented

Whether Petitioner was derived of his right, under the Sixth and Fourteenth Amendments, to a trial by a 12-person jury when the defendant is charged with a serious felony? There are two other petitions raising the same question presented. See Parada v. United States, No. 25-166; Minor v. Florida, No. 24-7489. This case should at least be held pending resolution of those petitions.
