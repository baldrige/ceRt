In Re Michael David Hower

IFP petition

Certiorari denied · October 3, 2022

Questions presented

  1. Has AEDPA created an unconstitutional barrier that has rendered the petitioners remedy under 28 U.S.C. §2255 inadequate or ineffective to test the legality of his detention by allowing the district court to refuse to hold an'T.evidentiary hearing or other inquiry that would allow him to develop a record to support his ineffective assistance of counsel claims when evidence exists outside the scope of the record in conversations between • the petitioner and his attorney?

  2. Is development of the record mandated by this courts case law authority in Massaro v. United States, 538 U.S. 500 (2003), Blackledge v. Allison, 52 LED 2D 136, 431 US 63 and 28 U.S.C. §2255(b) in order to conduct a proper "on the merits" review of the petitioners ineffective assistance of counsel claims when there exists evidence to support those claims outside of the scope of the record?

  3. In pleading guilty, does a criminal defendant inherently waive the right to move for withdrawal of his guilty plea before sentencing as allowed by Fed. R. Crim. P. 11(d)(2)(b)? (u > RELIEF SOUGHT -THE FACTS ON WHICH THIS MOTION IS BASED ARE ESTABLISHED IN HCWERS1 AFFIDAVIT. (Appendix B) and (Docket #103 & 104) Petitioner Michael David Hower prays for a writ of mandamus or as other­ wise appropriate, directed to the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the Honorable Robert J. Jonker, directing and commanding him to grant Mr. Hower a full and fair judicial review of of claims that he was coerced by the AUSA and his attorney to plead guilty to a crime he did not commit and his attorneys’ refusal to move for withdrawl of his guilty plea before sentencing, by holding an evidentiary hearing to develop a factual record bearing precisely on the issue created as required by this court in Blackledge v. Allison, 52 LED 2D 136, 431 US 63. UNAVAILABILITY OF RELIEF IN OTHER COURTS No other court will grant the relief sought by this petitioner because doing such would allow him to prove his innocence and bring attention to the criminal action by the AUSA and his attorneys and would show the flaws of the criminal justice system. In late 2011, Mr.Hower filed a 28 U.S.C. §2255, were he swore under. penalty of perjury that he was innocent of all the charges and only plead guilty to the AUSA’s "off the record” promise of 15 years, because he was coerced by him and his attorney Nunzio, who told him his wife was going to be arrested (absent probable cause), his children were going into state foster care, he was going to get life in Federal prison and face similar state ■ > charges. Attorney Nunzio told him to lie to the judge during the plea hearing and made him study the charging documents so he could convince the judge of .his guilt. Nunzio told him to deny that he was coerced or that there were any clandestine prosecutorial promises. Mr.Hower also claimed he ordered his atto­ rneys three times before sentencing to withdraw his guilty plea because he was innocent, but they refused. Mr.Hower has filed many motions to the district court and the court of appeals related to this case, a motion to; unseal docket #57, enforce FOIA, under rule 60(b), withdraw the guilty plea, 5 motions under 28 USG §2255 and one for a writ of mandamus, all were misconstrued and dismissed with no review. And now, 5 motions, to this United States Supreme Court. Mr.Hower has filed motions in other courts related to this case with the same results, he has filed a motion under 28 USC §2241 to the District Court in Maryland, but was dismissed because a §2241 is to challenge the manner in which the sentence is executed, not to challenge validity of the conviction and a civil action against his attorneys for malpractice in the District Court in Marquette, Michigan, hoping the court would order his two attorneys to repsond to the allegations of coercion and ineffective assistance of counsel made in his affidavit.(Appendix B) UNSUITABILITY OF ANY OTHER FORM OF RELIEF No other form of relief will be sufficient to protect the rights of the petitioner because the lower courts are acting prejudicial against him due to the nature of his charges and the allegations or wrong-doing by the AUSA and his attorneys. The district court was required by 28 USC §2255(b) to hold a hearing to allow Mr.Hower to further develop develop his claims of coercion and ineff­ ective assistance of counsel and then answer his §2255 petition. They know that the details of private attorney client conversations held outside the court room will not be on the record for them to review. They reviewed the §2255 without all of the facts, which made it easy for them to deny, because if the claims were proven true, he would most certainly be entitled to relief from his conviction and expose a serious flaw in the court proceedings. The lower court's also claim that Mr.Hower's 2012 §2255 was resolved on the merits, but where on the record are there any statements from his attorneys confirming or denying the claims of coercion or plea withdrawl? Because there is no evidence on the record, no court can do a legal on the merits review. Exceptional circumstances warrant the exercise of this courts discretionary power and to preserve the ability to seek review of the lower court's decision in this court, when it comes to a claim of actual innocence. The lower courts know that a pro se indigent defendant with this type of charge, has less than 1% chance of review with this court and utilized that knowledge to decide this case by ignoring the petitioners arguments, Federal Law, this courts case law authority and even their own. This petitioner has suffered irreparable harm over the past 12 years, not only is he being deprived of his life and liberty for a crime he did not commit, but it has also deprived his family. His loving Grandparents died without being able to say good-bye and his children have grown-up without \ a Father. If this court does not step in and mandate all parties involved to make at the mininum ANDERS briefs to develop the record, an innocent man will be sitting in prison for another 19 years.
