Clarence B. Jenkins, Jr. v. Office of South Carolina Governor, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, No. 26-1087 · judgment March 31, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Did the U.S. District Court, Columbia Division decision in dismissing lawsuit for Civil rights Violations per USEEOC Charge NO. 436-2025- 00781 which authorized litigation in U.S. Federal Court.

  2. Did the U.S. District Court, Columbia Division decision to dismiss lawsuit without notifying Defendant(s) for Civil Rights Violations as outlined USEEOC Charge No. 436-2025-00781 that authorized litigation in U.S. Federal Court.

  3. Did Pro Se Plaintiff received Due Process by U.S. District Court, Columbia Division to address Civil Rights Violations as stated in USEEOC Charge 436-2025-00781 require by the Fifth(5th) and Fourteenth^ 4th) Amendment of the U.S. Constitution

  4. Is it lawful for Civil Rights Violations by State and Local Government to to seek Redress through the U.S. Federal Court when U.S. Federal Violations has been committed
