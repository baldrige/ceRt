Jason Correa v. United States

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 16-2316 · judgment November 5, 2018

Certiorari denied · December 9, 2019
Pre-decision estimate: 1% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

Whether or under what circumstances the Fourth Amendment permits law enforcement agents to conduct a warrantless search of the electronic signal of an individual’s garage door remote and key fob, as well as use a key, seized from the person at the time of arrest, and utilize it to conduct a warrantless search to locate person’s apartment building, enter its secured common area, and utilize the key to locate the person’s apartment unit.
