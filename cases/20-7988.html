Robert Allen Poyson v. Arizona

IFP petition · Supreme Court of Arizona, No. CR-98-0510-AP · judgment November 2, 2020

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021

Questions presented

  1. Is the individualized capital sentencing requirement guaranteed by the Eighth Amendment violated when a State––whether by statute or courtimposed rule––predetermines that in all cases only minimal weight is to be afforded non-statutory mitigation, alleged to lack a causal nexus to the capital offense?

  2. Whether the Arizona Supreme Court’s review of a death sentence satisfies the Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments where it fails to consider whether the State has proved an aggravating factor?
