Questions presented

  1. Does a defendant who has been handcuffed,informed that there is a indictment for his arrest,questioned by the arresting officer,asked by the arresting officer if the defendant would like to help them out and work for them,and help himself by helping them,have a Constitutional Right to be read or advised of his Miranda Warnings?(the law states that a defendant is to be advised of his Miranda Warnings before any questioning and when the defendant has been handcuffed and is not free to leave and before defendant is interrogated).

  2. Does a defendant have the right to be served with the Search Warrant on the night that said motel room is searched,and does the defendant or the motel manger have to give consent to search the motel room,and is the defendant by law suppose to be given a a copy of what was seized from the motel room,on the night that the motel room was searched?

  3.   4. If the plea agreement was accepted because of the time that was being offered in the sentencing guidelines of the oral plea agreement,is the defendant entitled to said plea agreement?

  5.   8.
