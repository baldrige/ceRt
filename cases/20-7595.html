Randy T. Thomas v. Steve Stanson, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, No. 20-1068 · judgment August 24, 2020

Certiorari denied · June 1, 2021

Question presented

The Petitioner asks the court to decide if the Petitioner’s protections under the provisions of the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) where violated by Petersburg Utility Lines Water Department in its determination to terminate the Petitioner’s employment, without due process, during the timeframe that is substantiated by documented and approved FMLA leave request based on disability.
