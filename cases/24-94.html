Question presented

Chapter 11 reorganization plans often contain binding consensual releases. See, e.g., In re Specialty Equip. Cos., 3 F.3d 1043, 1047 (7th Cir. 1993). Non-consensual releases of non-debtor third parties are invalid. See Harrington v. Purdue Pharma L.P., 144 S. Ct. 2071 (2024). Attorneys generally may not contract with their clients to prospectively release claims for the attorneys’ malpractice. See, e.g., Model Rules of Prof’l Conduct R. 1.8(h) (2018).
