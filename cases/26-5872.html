Christopher C. Pinkston v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, No. 21-13391 · judgment June 12, 2026

Pending

Question presented

Federal sentencing courts often must determine whether a defendant’s prior state conviction qualifies as a predicate offense for recidivist enhancements—such as the Career-Offender Guideline (U.S.S.G. §4B1.1) or the Armed Career Criminal Act (ACCA). This case presents the recurrent and important issue that arises when a state supreme court issues a decision that changes the scope of a predicate offense in a way that would alter whether that prior conviction qualifies as a career-offender predicate at a later federal sentencing. The question presented for review is: Whether, when applying the Career Offender enhancement (U.S.S.G. §4B1.1), a federal sentencing court must determine a prior state conviction's qualification as a predicate offense by reference to state decisional law as it existed at the time of the prior offense, or whether it may instead rely on a later authoritative state court decision that reinterprets the scope of the prior offense.
