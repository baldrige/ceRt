Larry St. Clair Whetstone v. Fraley and Schilling Trucking Company

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit, No. 22-1018 · judgment September 28, 2022

Certiorari denied · June 5, 2023

Question presented

This is a case that strictly involves documentary evidence and not testimonial evidence. It is a very basic case that has been developed into a case being denied fair and equal treatment as it is so expressed in the “oath” that judges are sworn to uphold. The oath as it pertains to our justice system is: I, do solemnly swear or (affirm) that I will administer justice without respect to persons and do equal right to the poor and to the rich, and that I will faithfully and impartially discharge and perform all the duties incumbent upon me as_____under the constitution and laws of the United States. “So, help me GOD” The reason why this case is so important is because it involves civil rights and liberties outlined within the US Constitution being violated, and enabling a company to not have to answer to important merits of the case, because of a judge’s decision of discretion that doesn’t even come close to honoring the oath by which judges are sworn to uphold for all Americans of the United States. The questions presented, are as follows: • Is the decision of the Third Circuit Court of Appeals to uphold the lower court’s decision remain a decision that contradicts the judicial oath of fairness that judges are sworn to uphold? • Does the district court’s decision show bias unfair treatment based upon the choices the judge had before her and the fact that the defense didn’t follow the LCvR56 either? Is the main fact of the defense able to withstand in the court of law? Has the plaintiff provided supportive undisputable evidence beyond 50% preponderance that F&S indeed terminated his employment wrongfully which has violated his ADA/ADAA right as a matter of law.
