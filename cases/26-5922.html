E. S. v. L. J.

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Ohio, Summit County, No. 31614 · judgment March 18, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

Under the Fourteenth Amendment's Due Process Clause, state court procedures that extinguish a parent's right to seek judicial review of a judicial officer's decision affecting child protective interests before that decision takes legal effect violate fundamental fairness. The questions presented are:

  1. Whether the Due Process Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment prohibits a state trial court from entering a simultaneous, same-second judicial adoption of a magistrate's decision, where such immediate entry eliminates any meaningful opportunity for a parent to invoke state-created objection procedures before the order becomes enforceable.

  2. Whether the Due Process Clause is satisfied when a state appellate court dismisses a parent's constitutional procedural challenge as "premature" while state trial machinery operates to execute the unreviewed magistrate order, creating an unreviewable procedural impasse.
