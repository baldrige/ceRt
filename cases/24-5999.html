The Complaint #22-1181 makes a financial claim for Violation of the Petitioner's Civil Rights under "Biven Action Law". This Complaint #22-1181 is for the professional misconduct of the then US Attorney of PR Guillermo Gil Bonar and his main assistant AUSA Rebecca Kellogg indicated in Certiorari #21-6910 by the Petitioner, which the Federal Department of Justice WAIVED on January 26, 2022 to respond based on Rule 15(1H2) of the Federal Supreme Court. The Government's first response to Complaint #22-1181 was to request its summary dismissal under Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6) and that the Court classified the Petitioner as a "vexatious litigant". The Court did not heed the Government's request and ordered it to respond to Complaint #22-1181. After having requested three extensions of time to respond to Complaint #22-1181. the Government, on the deadline to respond, once again submitted the Motion to Dismiss with the request to declare the Petitioner a "vexatious litigant", which was honored by the Court in June 2023. On August 31,-2023, Petitioner filed Appeal #23-1640 with the First Circuit where he requested reversal of the Judgment issued in Complaint #22-1181. The Government after having been granted 4 extensions of time that include over 110 days to file their Opposition Brief to Appeal #23-1640. decide to file on the deadline of February 7, 2024 a Motion to Dismiss the Appeal. In the Motion the Government argues that the Supreme Court in the Case Heck v. Humprey established that a conviction must be invalidated for a financial claim to proceed and in the case of the Appellant there is no such invalidation and therefore the Complaint must be dismissed. Appeal #23-1640 was dismissed in the Order of Court of August 19, 2024 where the requested Rehearing is denied.
