Eddie Lee Jackson v. Sandy McCain, Warden

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 18-30692 · judgment February 1, 2019

Certiorari denied · June 3, 2019

Question presented

Whether the Petitioner was denied the right to assistance of counsel when the counsel representing Petitioner throughout the proceedings was in fact suspended from the practice of law in the state of Louisiana for misconduct? Whether the Petitioner was denied the right to effective assistance of counsel when counsel performed his duties under ill medical condition causing his performance to fall well below the standard guaranteed by the 6 11 and 14th Amendments? Whether the court erred by failing to appoint the Petitioner new counsel after his counsel passed away at a critical juncture in the proceedings?
