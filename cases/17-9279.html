Emem Ufot Udoh v. Becky Dooley, Warden

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit, No. 17-2695 · judgment February 8, 2018

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

Petitioner presented these six Grounds for review in his first Writ of Certiorari. Udoh v. Minnesota, No. 16-5133, 137 S. Ct. 185, 196 L. Ed 2d 151 (2016). Petitioner presented the same six Grounds, including ineffective assistance of both trial and appellate counsels claims for habeas relief and certificate of appealability ('COX') at the federal district court. Ground One was whether expert testimony which embraces legal conclusion on ultimate issue of fact interfered with Petitioner's right to a fair trial. Ground Two was regarding the remedy for Petitioner's double jeopardy clause violations. Ground Three was whether Petitioner's federal constitutional rights are violated in state court evidentiary rulings that excluded evidence of the complainants' prior false allegations and conducts from adverse and defense witnesses. Ground Four was whether the erroneous admission of evidence obtained without the provisions of the Fourth, Sixth and Fourteenth Amendments denied Petitioner a fundamentally fair trial and whether the cumulative effects of Prosecutorial misconducts in this case deprived Petitioner's constitutional right to a fair trial. Ground Five was on Petitioner's motion for judgment of acquittal on the grounds of insufficient evidence to sustain the wrongful convictions and jury instructional errors. The question(s) presented is as follows: 1. Did the federal court exceed its authority under the Antiterrorism and Effective Death Penalty Act ("AEDPA") by denying habeas relief on the Grounds and Claims above?
