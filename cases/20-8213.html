James Atwood v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 20-2794 · judgment April 30, 2021

Certiorari denied · January 10, 2022

Question presented

Does the undefined term “controlled substance” in the federal Sentencing Guidelines mean substances controlled by federal law, the federal Controlled Substances Act (CSA)—or, does the term also refer to the myriad definitions found in each state law, such that prior convictions from states with drug schedules broader than the federal CSA qualify as predicate “controlled substance offenses” for purposes of the career-offender enhancement? The same question is currently pending before this Court in Ward v. United States, No. 20-7327.
