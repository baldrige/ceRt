Jermel Pope v. Lake County Sheriff’s Department, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 25-2486 · judgment December 10, 2025

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Whether a federal court may dismiss a non-frivolous civil rights action with prejudice, without reaching the merits, based solely upon an indigent litigant’s alleged noncompliance with fee-related requirements under 28 U.S.C. § 1915, consistent with the First Amendment right of access to the courts and the Due Process Clause of the Fifth Amendment.

  2. Whether a court of appeals may deny leave to proceed in forma pauperis and dismiss an appeal for failure to pay the appellate filing fee where the appellant alleges that the underlying dismissal itself rested exclusively upon fee-related procedural grounds rather than any determination that the constitutional claims lacked merit.

  3. Whether this Court should grant certiorari to resolve the constitutional limits governing dismissal of indigent civil actions on procedural fee grounds where no federal court has considered the merits of the petitioner’s constitutional claims.

  4. Whether summary reversal is warranted where lower courts dismissed a nonfrivolus constitutional act with prejudice in direct conflict with this Court’s controlling precedent, without conducting any merit review.
