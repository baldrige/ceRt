IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit, No. 18-5093 · judgment April 6, 2018

Certiorari denied · June 18, 2018

Question presented

: Is The Supreme Court of The United States permitted to put to trial the President for possible grounds for filing as laid out in the originating cases 3: 17-cv-00846 U.S. District Court Middle District of Tennessee and United States Court of Appeals For The Sixth Circuit Case 18-5093? May any of the The United States Court findings be sent to the United States Congress for potential Impeachment hearings? (Ill) Whereas the original motion to reopen the case was filed and entered on November 28th, 2017 which was within 180 days from the District Court Order closing the District Case 3:17-cv-00846 on June 26, 2017 See 28 U.S. Code § 2107 may the case on appeal be heard in The Supreme Court of The United States? (TV) Whereas all three Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals Presiding Judges were nominated by Republican Presidents [gives an appearance of (possible) prejudice See 28 U.S. Code § 2107 as the Defendant is also a Republican President] may the case on appeal be heard in The Supreme Court of The United States?
