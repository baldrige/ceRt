Question presented

The Fair Labor Standards Act (“FLSA”), 29 U.S.C. § 201 et seq., guarantees a minimum and overtime wage to covered “employees.” The question presented is: Whether inmates working in furtherance of public works projects for the government charged with their custody and care may qualify as “employees” under the FLSA.
