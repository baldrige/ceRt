Michele DiCosola v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 16-3497 · judgment August 14, 2017

Certiorari denied · May 29, 2018

Question presented

PETITION FOR A WRIT OF CERTIORARI WANT AND LACK OF SUBJECT MATTER JURISDICTION Is the district court in want and lack of subject matter jurisdiction, prosecution, and warrant, from both the Grand Jury and Department of Justice, when both the Foreperson and U.S. Attorney failed or refused to sign and disclose the original indictment from the time of arrest and through sentencing, causing plan error under Rule 52(b) for violation of substantive rights under Rule 6(c) and 7(c)(1), which is in compliance with the 5th and 6th Amendment of the Constitution’s “Due Process Clause” and “Notice for Nature and Cause Clause” for felonies accused of? TRUE BILL OF INDICTMENT WITHOUT SWORN DATE UNCONSTITUTIONAL Is an indictment without a sworn date affixed to it afterit is signed by the U.S. Attorney, Foreperson or both, unconstitutional if not dated and filed as Docket No. 1, in order to prevent the invitation of abuse from both the Grand Jury and or Prosecutor through involuntary or unknown waiver of indictment and probable cause hearing and or the backdating of said indictment in favor of the prosecution case? STANDARD WHEN CUMULATIVE HARMLESS ERRORS CONVERT TO PLAIN ERROR What is the standard for review on loss of jurisdiction or new trial when more than one harmless error is grounds for conversion to plain error? USURPATION OF JURY FACT FINDER THROUGH USE OF PROBATION OFFICER WITNESS HEARSAY TESTIMONY IN VIOLATION OF APPRENDI RULE, 5TH, 6TH, AND 7TH AMENDMENTS Can a non-judicial probation officer provide hearsay testimony of restitution, without affidavit, first hand knowledge and sworn testimony and or any documentation to support the monetary injury claimed, in order for the district court to adopt both the monetary injury total and sentencing guidelines, while facts contested are at issue, without being in violation of Apprendi Rule, 5th Amendment “Due Process Clause”, 6th Amendment “Confrontation Clause” and “Compulsory Clause”, and the 7th Amendment “Value in Controversy” amd “Re-examination of Facts According to Common Law” Clauses? SUPERSESSIONS AND UNCONSTITUTIONALITY ISSUE(S). OF CRIMINAL JUSTICE ACT VS ALL WRITS ACT AND 7TH AMENDMENT Does the CJA cause a supersession issue with the All Writs Act in addition to it being simultaneously unconstitutional and in direct conflict with the supervisory powers of the one supreme court, the 1st, 6th and 7th Amendment(s), when petitioner invokes his right to “Petition for Redress of Grievances” on direct criminal appeal to the Supreme Court’s original jurisdiction for re-examination of facts in a common law court with request for assistance of counsel, but access to this Court under CJA is restricted only to Certiorari, abrogating both the All Writs Act and the supervisory and discretionary powers of the original jurisdiction, knowing that misappropriations of facts will rarely be reviewed Certiorari? ABUSE AND AVOIDANCE OF SUPERVISORY POWERS Is avoidance of supervisory power from appeal court abuse of discretion when under direct criminal appeal, questions of prosecutorial abuse, abuse of discretion, and a collateral attach of an injunction on the merits related to a challenge of subject matter jurisdiction in the district court are completely discarded and ignored without de novo as if complicit in sanctioning the lower court’s abuse?
