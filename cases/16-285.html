Epic Systems Corporation v. Jacob Lewis

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit, No. 15-2997 · judgment May 26, 2016

Decided · May 21, 2018
Pre-decision estimate: 2% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

Consolidated with 16-300. Whether an agreement that requires an employer and an employee to resolve employment-related disputes through individual arbitration, and waive class and collective proceedings, is enforceable under the Federal Arbitration Act, notwithstanding the provisions of the National Labor Relations Act.
