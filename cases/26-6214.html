Peter J. Barclay v. Daniel R. Murphy, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 26-715 · judgment August 25, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

Congress assigned selection of a child’s representative payee to the Social Security Administration (SSA) and apportionment of veterans’ compensation to the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). The state court based an $800 monthly obligation to the custodial parent on petitioner’s receipt of his daughter’s benefit while he remained her SSA representative payee, and separately set spousal payments based on his sole income, VA compensation and SSDI.

  1. Whether the exception to judicial immunity for acts performed in the clear absence of all jurisdiction applies to state judges’ commands that perform functions Congress reserved to SSA and VA: (a) converting receipt of a child’s own Social Security benefits into her representative payee’s personal obligation to pay a courtselected recipient; and (b) determining under state criteria another person’s eligibility for and recurring share of VA disability compensation included in the veteran’s identified VA/SSDI income pool.

  2. Whether dismissal with prejudice of an entire pro se civil-rights action, followed by dismissal of its appeal as frivolous, is proper where petitioner disputes the application of judicial immunity and limitations to the challenged conduct, the complaint also incorporates a separate January 2026 encounter alleging an unlawful search and threats by armed officers during his efforts to report alleged criminal conduct and seek official action, and the court affords no opportunity to cure substantive pleading deficiencies that amendment could potentially remedy.
