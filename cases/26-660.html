Questions presented

The Seventh Amendment preserves the right of trial by jury in suits at common law. That right includes the right to defend against penalties asserted by administrative agencies like the IRS. But the Fifth Circuit below held that taxpayers cannot vindicate that right because of the Tax Anti-Injunction Act in 26 U.S.C. § 7421(a). This case presents an ideal vehicle to address this issue and resolve two questions of exceptional importance:

  1. Whether the Tax Anti-Injunction Act precludes taxpayers from bringing direct actions to vindicate their constitutional rights.

  2. Whether taxpayers have a constitutional right to a jury trial when the IRS asserts penalties based on misstatements and negligence.
