Ramon Rios, III v. Texas

Paid petition · Court of Appeals of Texas, Fourteenth District, No. 14-18-00886-CR · judgment August 27, 2020

Certiorari denied · October 3, 2022
Pre-decision estimate: 1% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

I. Does the Fourth Amendment prohibit a warrantless protective sweep of the interior of a home following the arrest of a resident outside his home that had been under 24-hour surveillance for more than two months? II. Can the plain-view doctrine provide an exception to the warrant requirement when items are stored in opaque black trash bags that law enforcement officers observed while conducting a 30-minute protective sweep of the interior of a home after completing the arrest of the resident outside his home?
