Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 15-56220, 15-56221 · judgment October 2, 2017

Certiorari denied · May 29, 2018
Pre-decision estimate: 16% cert probability (95% interval 11%–22%)

Before the decision, about 3.9× the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for counsel who has won certiorari before, a Ninth Circuit decision below, and a circuit split argued in the petition, and down for a business respondent.

Question presented

Whether a person who obtains funds from a debtor and exercises complete control over the funds, but lacks the legal authority to do so, is an “initial transferee” under Section 550(a)(1) of the Bankruptcy Code. (I)
