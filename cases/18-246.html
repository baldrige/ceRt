Mary L. Doherty, et al. v. Allstate Indemnity Company

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit, No. 17-1860 · judgment May 25, 2018

Certiorari denied · December 3, 2018
Pre-decision estimate: 8% cert probability (95% interval 5%–12%)

Before the decision, about 1.9× the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for a Third Circuit decision below, a circuit split argued in the petition, and a dissent in the court below (flagged in the petition), and down for a business respondent.

Question presented

Whether the affirmed Rule 56 summary judgment decision is inconsistent with the standard set forth in Anderson v. Liberty Lobby, Inc., 477 U.S. 242 (1986), and violative of the “Mend-the-Hold” Doctrine in Railway v. McCarthy, 96 U.S. 258 (1878), as the court relied upon inadmissible hearsay and simultaneously precluded relevant, material evidence of the insureds’ Expert Witness Reports thereby depriving them of their rights to Due Process and a Jury Trial under the Fifth and Seventh Amendments.
