Jodie Louise Byrne v. Maryland, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the First Circuit, No. 20-1568 · judgment November 16, 2021

Certiorari denied · October 3, 2022

Question presented

The lower courts have failed to apply the Laws of the United States. The USA holds a 90 percent failure rates in the State Courts regarding the Protection of Crime Victims after they report a violent crime, and the offender is often free to continue the abuse of his victims for life. Why? My cbristitutional rights have been decimated. My human rights have been ignored which violates numerous US laws, treaties as well as basic Human Rights laws. Under 42 US Code 1983 is regarding the Civil action for deprivation of rights. In Jogi v. Vogas 480 F 3.d 822 (7th Cir 2007) the Seventh Circuit decided that an individual may sue for damages under section 1983 when he or she is not informed of his right to consular notification. The Seventh Circuit began its analysis by concluding that the phrase “and laws” in section 183 should be read to include treaties (The Yale Journal of International Law Vol, 37:1). “Every person who, under color of any statue, ordinance, regulation, custom, or usage, of State or Territory or the District of Columbia, subjects or causes to be subjected, any citizen of the United States or other person within the jurisdiction thereof to the deprivation of any rights, privileges or immunities secured by the Constitution and laws, shall be liable to the party injured in an action at law, suite in equity, or other proper proceedingfor redress. ” Why is this court allowing Human Rights to be decimated under the 14th Amendment to the Constitution regarding due process and equal protection? The 1, 5,4,8 and 9are impacted as well. Numerous state actors violated House Concurrent Resolution 172. The Landmark Row Vs Wade is impacted. I have two people dead and five miscarriages that I know about already from the court’s failures, in my family. How many more must die before the court start following the laws of the USA?
