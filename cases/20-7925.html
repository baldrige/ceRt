In Re Clement Mosseri

IFP petition

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021

Questions presented

  1. Is petitioner entitled to a fair hearing before an unbiased tribunal?

  2. Does a poor person, Pro se litigant have a right to an attorney without costs if that tribunal discriminates and will not give a fair hearing unless he is represented by an attorney?

  3. Does the SDNY and its judges have a right to discriminate against litigants by having a separate process for unrepresented litigants they call Pro se Intake while attorneys have access to the Clerk’s Office in a separate building and where judges have different rules for pro se litigants than attorneys denying fundamental rights for unrepressed pro se litigants?
