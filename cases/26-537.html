Questions presented

At approximately 11:56 p.m. on June 13, 2019, Officers Shawn Jones and Logan Kulesa of the Jacksonville Police Department responded to a latenight burglary call at a Jacksonville car dealership after cleaning crew members reported an intruder inside the building. Officers discovered Tramon Savage inside the service department attempting to start vehicles. Despite officers identifying themselves and issuing repeated commands, Savage entered a truck and drove toward the open bay door. Officers perceived that Savage was attempting to strike officers with the vehicle and opened fire. After the truck crashed, Officer Kulesa briefly continued firing because he believed Savage was reaching for a weapon or other object. The shooting lasted less than five seconds. Officers then summoned medical assistance and rendered aid. Savage later died from his injuries. The questions presented are:

  1. Whether the Eighth Circuit wrongly denied qualified immunity to Officers Jones and Kulesa by finding that it did not have jurisdiction to reverse the District Court’s denial of qualified immunity based on its determination that there were disputed facts.

  2. Had the Eighth Circuit properly engaged the qualified immunity analysis, would it have found that Officers Kulesa and Jones violated clearly established law.
