B. Nicholas Velarde v. Joseph R. Biden, Jr., President of the United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit, No. 23-1465 · judgment November 14, 2023

Certiorari denied · May 13, 2024

Question presented

1). GIVEN THE POLITICAL STATUS OF THE EXISTING US PRESIDENT & COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF, HOW IS IT POSSIBLE THAT SOMEONE COULD BE ELECTED TO THE HIGHEST OFFICE IN THE LAND WHEN, IN FACT, HE DIDN’T WIN? 2). GEOPOLITICALLY SPEAKING, HOW DOES ONE DETERMINE THAT INFORMATION PRESENTED BEFORE THE COURT IS OR IS NOT RELEVANT? THAT, TO PROVE OUR POINT IT WAS REQUIRED TO GO BACK TO THE “POINT IN QUESTION” (EX OVER 100 YEARS AGO) DURING THE THIRD DEVELOPMENTAL PERIOD OF MODERN POLITICS? 3). IF A MANDATE IS THE NEXT STEP IN THE LEGAL PROCESS HOW THEN CAN THE CASE BE DECIDED ON THE FACTS ALREADY PRESENTED BEFORE THE COURT? 5). APPEALS COURTS DECISION WAS TAKEN OUT OF CONTEXT, AS RECORDED, HOW COULD THIS BE CORRECTED? “Velarde further alleges that he has suffered “terrible feelings of pain and hatred” due to President Biden’s and previous administrations’failures to remedy the injustices that occurred during the McKinley administration.” BUT OUR TESTIMONY WAS IN RELATIONSHIP TO MY RELATIONSHIP WITH MY FATHER2, “The notion that, as time elapses, social, political and economic differences between immigrants and natives fade...” but they didn’t as my relationship with him was filled with so much hatred and anger I vowed to do something about it.” 6). Regarding grounds for sua sponte dismissal (SEE BELOW) APPEALS COURT STATED, “when allegations of a complaint are totally implausible, attenuated, unsubstantiated, frivolous, devoid of merit, or no longer open to discussion.” BUT TO PROCEED IN A FORWARD MANNER HOW IS IT NOW POSSIBLE TO ADVANCE THE WORLD INTO ITS FINAL DEVELOPMENTAL PERIOD?3 2 My Father: On Earth as He is in Heaven COPYRIGHTED MATERIAL 7). HOW IS THE ISRAEL-PALESTINE WAR, OR PALESTINE REVOLUTION A MANIFESTATION OF THE THIRD DEVELOPMENTAL PERIOD, OR COLONIAL REVOLUTION OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY? 8). HOW CAN THE WORLD RESET/RESTRUCTURE THE DEBT4 (INTERNAL & EXTERNAL) OF THE EXISTING US BASED GLOBAL ECONOMY IN AND OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY? 4 USDEBTCLOCK.ORG
