Stuart Force, et al. v. Facebook, Inc.

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, No. 18-397 · judgment July 31, 2019

Certiorari denied · May 18, 2020
Pre-decision estimate: 9% cert probability (95% interval 6%–14%)

Question presented

Section 230(c)(1) of Title 47 states that no provider of interactive computer service (such as a website) “shall be treated as the publisher of any information provided by another information content provider [such as a user who posts something on the website].” The questions presented are:

  1. Is section 230(c)(1) a limitation on the definition of a publisher under certain other prohibitions, or a broad grant of immunity to covered publishers, and

  2. Is “publisher” in section 230(c)(1) limited to the exercise of traditional editorial functions, such as deciding to accept or reject a submission? The same questions are presented in Dyroff v. The Ultimate Software Group, No. 19-___. The petition in Dyroff is being filed simultaneously with the petition in the instant case.
