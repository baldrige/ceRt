In Re Ohio, ex rel. Terpsehore P. Maras

Paid petition

Certiorari denied · January 9, 2023
Pre-decision estimate: 0% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

The question presented is whether Ohio law allowing for Republican and Democratic Parties' election observers in any Ohio precinct or board of elections while requiring that statewide Independent candidates obtain consent from four other candidates before being allowed to appoint election. observers violates the Equal Protection Clause of the 14th Amendment of the Constitution of the United States.
