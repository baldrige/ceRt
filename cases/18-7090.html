Leonardo T. Morales v. Florida

IFP petition · Supreme Court of Florida, No. SC18-1219 · judgment August 28, 2018

Dismissed · February 19, 2019

Question presented

I "Whether the Florida Supreme Court dismissal of mandamus conflicts with this court's decisions in Schlagenhaufv. Holder, 379 U.S. 104, 110, 85 S. Ct. 234, 13 L. Ed. 2D 152 (1964); Bankers Life and Casualty Co. v. Holland, 346 U.S. 379, 383, 74 S. Ct. 145, 98 L. Ed. 106 (1953); Cheney v. U.S. Dist. Court, 542 U.S. 367, 380-382,124 S. Ct. 2576, 159 L. Ed. 2D 459 (2004). II "Whether such dismissal violates Petitioner's right to due process, access to courts and it is tantamount to suspension of the Writ of Habeas Corpus?" Missouri PR. Co. v. Clarendon Boat Oar Co., 257 U.S. 533, 535, 42 S. Ct. 210, 66 L. Ed. 354 (1922); Bounds v. Smith, 430 U.S. 817, 824, 828, 97 S. Ct. 1491, 52 L. Ed. 2d 72 (1977) overruled in part by Lewis v. Casey, 518 U.S. 343, 116 S. Ct. 2174, 135 L. Ed. 2d 606 (1996); and Boumediene v. Bush, 533 U.S. 723, 128 S. Ct. 2229, 2240, 171 L. Ed. 2D 41(2008).
