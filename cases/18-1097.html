Questions presented

  1. Is a state law exempt from the Dormant Commerce Clause merely because it does not discriminate against interstate commerce?

  2. Is a state law exempt from the Dormant Commerce Clause merely because Congress has passed a federal statute saving the law from preemption under that statute?
