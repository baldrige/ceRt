Sandra Sue Grazzini-Rucki v. David Victor Rucki

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Minnesota, No. A16-1970 · judgment August 21, 2017

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

Did the magistrate commit reversible error by failing to commence modification of support retroactive to the filing of themotion, and by making the modified support order subject to review nunc pro tunc violative of Minn. Stat. 518A.39 (2)(A)(Modification of Orders)? In a February 1 Order, the Magistrate ruled that the modified support commence January], even though the Appellant’s Motion to Modify Support had been filed and Served on July 29, and again on November 17 (ADD. 14) Each Magistrate issued modified support orders nunc pro tunc. Apposite Authority: Minn. Stat. 518A.39 (2)(A) Did the Magistrate commit reversible error by failing to make adverse inferences when calculating a parent’s gross income in contravention of Minn. Stat. 518A.28 resulting in monetary child support’ against an indigent parent? The Magistrate confined its determination ofgross income to paycheck stubs and ignored evidence of additional sources of income and disposing of assets and income; while imputing income to an indigent parent. Apposite Authority: Minn. Stat. 518A.28; §518A.28(a) (Providing Income Information); Spooner v. Spooner, 410 N.W.2d 412,413 (Minn. App. 1987) Did the Magistrate commit reversible error by a temporary order assessing a minimum amount of child support against an indigent parent with no determination of basic support and no analysis of ability to pay and to support herself? pursuant to Minn. Stat. 518A.42 After a ruling that a change in circumstances warranted a modfIcation of support, the Magistrate set support temporarily to zero, and another Magistrate assessed a minimum child support amount pending an evidentiary hearing, even though Appellant was on public assistance and had no income. Apposite Authority: Minn. Stat. §518A.42; 518A.29 (h) Did the Magistrate commit’reversible error when it obstruted a parent’s right to modify child support authorized by Minn. Stat. 518A.32 by assessing potential income to support a monetary award against an indigent jarent in contravention of Minn. Stat. 518A.32 (Potential Income), 518A.39 (Modification of Orders) and 518 A.42 (Ability to Pay; Self Support)?
