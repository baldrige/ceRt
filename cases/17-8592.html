Humberto Rodriguez, Jr. v. Lorie Davis, Director, Texas Department of Criminal Justice. Correctional Institutions Division

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 16-41176 · judgment December 20, 2017

Certiorari denied · June 25, 2018

Question presented

QUESTION ONE: Is, U.S. V. Cotton, 122 S.Ct. 1781(2002) stating: (Defects in subject matter jurisdiction can never be forfeited or waived, requires correction, regardless of whether error was raised in District Court), a substantive rule of law as described by, Montgomery V. Louisiana, 136 S.Ct. 718(2016), which states a substantive error is something a state does that is not allowed by law? And is that jurisdiction challenge exempt from federal habeas time-bar? QUESTION TWO: Is an indictment void from its inception that alleges subject matter jurisdiction as: ... in Hidalgo County, Texas, in the course of attempting to commit and committing kidnapping, did then and there intentionally commit murder..., if the state knew beforehand and proved at trial the murder occurred in the country of Mexico? And is that a substantive error of due process that is exempt from time-bar and requiring correction? QUESTION THREE: Is it a substantive violation of federal and Constitutional law for a state to assume extraterritorial" jurisdiction over a murder occurring in Mexico using a State "Territorial" statute? flUESTION FOUR: Is it a substantive due process error in a capital murder case for a state to lower its burden of proof in the jury charge by changing the capital murder statute's requirement of intentionally or knowingly, to should have known someone else would commit murder? QUESTION FIVE: Does overwhelming proof of actual innocence provided in a 28 U.S.C. §2254 habeas corpus "require" review on the merits notwithstanding AEDPA procedural and time-bar limitations as the Supreme Court ruled in, McQuiggin V. Perkins, 133 S.Ct. 1924(2013), stating; "Actual innocence,, if proven, serves as a gateway through which a petitioner may pass whether impediment is a procedural bar... or expiration of the statute of limitations?"
