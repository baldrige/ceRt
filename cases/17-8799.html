Nelson Zeledon v. Shawn Hatton, Warden

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 17-16145 · judgment January 31, 2018

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

Does Trial Attorney’s admittance of an error so egregious that it undermines the outcome of trial, of one whom is actually innocent, sufficient to meet the threshold set forth in Stricklandv. Washington, 466 U.S. 668, 688 (1984), for Ineffective Assistance of Counsel? If So? Petitioner received Ineffective Assistance of counsel when trial counsel failed to object to the use of tape recordings in which Petitioner’s wife recounted private conversations she had with her husband which would implicate him if missinterpreted and taken out of context, as they were by the Prosecution, after they had closed their case and the Defense openned theirs, in violation of Evidence Code Section 980, and relying on confidential marital communication, in order to sway a dead-locked jury, And if Trial Counsel was ineffective should a new trial be granted? See: Appendix “B” (Defendant’s Motion For A New Trial CA. P.C. Sec. 1.181), or at minumum hbeas relief granted on this ground. The Issue Relating To A Juror’s Observation Of Petitioner’s Behavior While Not Testifying. Did the Trial Court err, and was defense counsel ineffective after a juror expressed the view that it did not seem “right” for Petitioner to “stare”Tht the complaining witness during her testimony, where the juror was not instructed that he could not consider Petitioner’s demeanor in Court other than when Petitioner was testifying?
