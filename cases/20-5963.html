Daniel Edward Murray v. Bobby Lumpkin, Director, Texas Department of Criminal Justice, Correctional Institutions Division

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 19-40273 · judgment July 20, 2020

Certiorari denied · December 14, 2020

Questions presented

  1. If there is a set of plea agreements taken at the same time and the court of appeals reverses a part of the appealed agreeemnt does this, A) Start the AEDPA time for the entire agreement over or B) Start the AEDPA time at the time at the end of the appealed portion or C) Allow the indigent, pro se lititgant to appeal under AEDPA the only the portion that was reversed?

  2. When should the pro se, litigant calculate the time as beginning?

  3. Does the HIPAA law prevent court appointed attorneys from handing over defendant medical records to the prosecution without their permission?

  4. Was trial counsel ineffective for recommending a “rehabilitation” facility for a psychological opinion without doing an investigation of the facility?

  5. Trial counsel admitted that he had four years to prepare for trial, but was he ineffective for failing to attempt to exclude the report made by the psychological facility?
