Daniel Clemans v. Kentucky

IFP petition · Supreme Court of Kentucky, No. 2024-SC-0223-MR · judgment June 25, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

  1. ) Does Courts’ enforcement of “Marsy’s Law” (and permitting separation of witness ORDER violations) transgress Supremacy Clause and Accuseds’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law and Sixth Amendment right to a Fair trial, effective cross-examination/ confrontation, when said “enforcement” nullifies Accuseds’ Federal Constitutional protections ? 2.) Does a Reviewing Court of last resort disregarding law enforcement’s intentional destroying / discarding of admittedly-exonerating/exculpatory Forensic Evidence BEFORE turning it over to Prosecution or Defendant violate Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Sixth Amendment fair trial protections and in utter conflict with relevant long-held U.S. Supreme Court decisions ?

  2. ) Does a Reviewing Court of last resort disregarding a Detective’s suppression of his demanded “to-be-preserved” investigatory Notebook containing contemporaneously-noted atthe-scene exonerating/exculpatory eye-witnesses’ Statements, contact information, and impeachment evidence, Never turning it over to Prosecution or Defendant, violate a Citizen’s Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Sixth Amendment fair trial protections, and in utter conflict with relevant long-held U.S. Supreme Court decisions ?

  3. ) Does a Reviewing Court of last resort violate Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Sixth Amendment fair trial protections when it disregards PROVEN and Cited Prosecutorial misconduct of allowing knowingly-false Material testimony to jurors to go uncorrected then actually USING said false testimony in closing argument to wrongfully convict, in utter conflict with relevant long-held U.S. Supreme Court decisions, especially Napue v, Illinois (infra) ?

  4. ) To what extent is intentional flagrant Prosecutorial Misconduct permissible during so-called “Wide-Latitude in Closing Arguments” before violating Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process protections and Sixth Amendment Fair Trial rights and in direct conflict with the relevant long-held decisions of the U.S. Supreme Court ?

  5. ) To what extent is a State allowed to offend Doctrine of Stare Decisis infringing upon Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Equal Protection of Law and Due Process when it disregards cited NON-overruled precedent that Citizens rely on and depend on in their exact circumstances ?

  6. ) To what extent are Courts permitted to disregard “The Supremacy Clause” of the United States by completely ignoring Cited (Adopted) relevant Federal Precedent before offending Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law ? 8.) Do State Courts of last resort infringe upon Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Equal Protection of Law and First Amendment guarantee to access the Court to redress grievances when they intentionally impede and deny without reason some litigants a word/page-limit increase enabling additional space for composing additional said grievances/Constitutional errors, thus forcing waiver/non-preservation of Citizens’ asserted Federal Constitutional errors committed by the very State, where “no law” shall abridge . .. petitioning “the Government for redress of grievances” ? 9.) Does a Court forcing surprise evidentiary hearings on Citizens concerning “serious loss” of “life and liberty,” with ZERO Notice or Meaningful opportunity AND denying continuance for said surprise hearings without reason, violate Citizens’ Fifth and Fourteenth Amendment Due Process protections and First Amendment access to courts and in conflict with relevant decisions of the U.S. Supreme Court ?

  7. ) Do State Courts violate Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Sixth Amendment fair trial protections when they permit medical-Expert testimony to jurors of conclusions in Law and intentional state-of-mind cause of death (i.e.: “assault”) as “ultimate fact”, conjunctive with said Expert’s straightforward lies, speculation, and testimony derived from UN-subpoenaed “colleagues” and zero methodology, contrary to scientific norms, and in direct conflict with Daubert. Smith v. Arizona, and Diazv. United States (infra) ?

  8. ) Do Courts violate Accuseds’ Sixth Amendment Fair Trial and Jury-Unanimity protection and Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law by allowing conviction by duplicitous Jury instruction embedded with an ambiguous and undefined Statutory crime (i.e.: intentional “Assault”) as causation to the alleged crime (i.e.: UNintentional “wanton murder”) without ANY “elements” or “definitions” (of “Assault”) for jurors to Rule Out, in conflict with relevant decisions of the U.S. Supreme Court ?

  9. ) Do Courts’ complete disregard and refusal of “competency” determination of a repeatedlyasserted incompetent child-witness violate Defendants’ Sixth Amendment fair trial and crossexamination and Confrontation Clause protections and Fourteenth Amendment Due Process/ Equal Protection of Law, conflicting with long-held Kentucky v. Stincer, 482 U.S. 730 (1987) ?

  10. ) Does allowing conviction of a Factually/Actually innocent person based on speculation, conjecture, guesswork, and grossly-insufficient evidence in a purely-circumstantial case instead of on actual “Elements” of alleged crime violate an Accused’s Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and in direct conflict with long held decisions of the U.S. Supreme Court ?

  11. ) Do States violate Litigants’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law and Sixth Amendment rights when they deny them its own State-protected (and Federalprotected) rights to self-representation, Forcing on Litigants State-Appointed Counsel on appeal as of State-Right and Forcing said Counsels’ “defective/deficient” Brief on Appellants ? 15.) Does United States Supreme Court “Rule 10(c)” offend Citizens’/Litigants’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process by encouraging/permitting Reviewing Courts to disregard or erroneously construe Exonerating I Exculpatory / Material facts, knowing Rule 10(c) “rarely” GRANTS Certiorari to Reviewing Courts for their “erroneous factual findings” ?

  12. ) Do State Reviewing Courts of last resort violate Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process protections and First Amendment right to redress grievances when they disregard clearly-cited indisputable Exonerating/Exculpatory “Material Facts” AND Evidence that demonstrate Actual/Factual Innocence, especially in fact-dependent purely-circumstantial cases ? 17.) Do Courts’ failures and refusals to view or “balance” Government’s proffered prejudicial evidence that is then submitted for Juror viewing violate Accuseds’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law and Sixth Amendment Fair Trial guarantees ?

  13. ) Do Courts violate Citizens’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process and Equal Protection of Law rights and Sixth Amendment self-representation rights when they refuse to liberally construe and have leniency on pro se filed documents and arguments who are not trained in law, in direct conflict and contravention to Estelle v. Gamble, 429 U.S. 97, 106 (1976) and Boag v. MacDougall. 454 U.S. 364. 365 (1982) ?

  14. ) Do Courts violate litigants’ Fourteenth Amendment Due Process rights and Equal Protection of Law when they intentionally disregard and refuse to take mandatory “Process” of Judicial Notice of provided adjudicative Material Facts, Evidence and PROOF that are indisputable, “accurate” and “cannot reasonably be questioned” and the “necessary information” provided ?
