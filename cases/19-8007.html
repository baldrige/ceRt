Gwendolyn Gabriel v. Merry Outlaw

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Texas, Fifth District, No. 05-17-01270-CV · judgment June 27, 2019

Certiorari denied · May 18, 2020

Questions presented

In 2010, the Defendant and 2 Plaintiffs purchased a house to rehabilitate. During the house*rehabilitation project by the Gay Defendant and the 2 Heterosexual Plaintiffs (also sisters), the Gay Defendant retaliated against one of the Plaintiffs (also known as the Petitioner) by withdrawing $600 from the joint account because the Heterosexual Plaintiff rejected the Gay Defendant’s unwanted sexual advances after the Plaintiff told her that she was going to use the $600 to buy the stucco materials. The SEXUAL HARASSMENT AND THIS RETALIATORY ACT caused i the Plaintiff to withdraw from the project. The following year, the Gay Defendant tricked the Co-Plaintiff into unknowingly signing over 26% of her share to the Gay Defendant. When Plaintiff found out and told Co-Plaintiff that she had actually signed a deed, the Co-Plaintiff filed a lawsuit against the Defendant in 2013 alleging fraud in a real estate transaction against Co-Plaintiff, Plaintiff was included “for partition purposes only,” because she owned l/3rd of the house. In response, the Defendant committed perjury in the Defendant’s Answer by stating that: l) it was Co-Plaintiff, not her, who withdrew the $600, and

  1. that it was Co-Plaintiff who went to the Defendant and knowingly and voluntarily deeded over her 26% of her share in the property. Because clear and convincing evidence reveals that the Defendant committed OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE BY PERJURY in her Defendant’s Amended Original Answer, Special Exceptions and Counterclaim, the following Issues are presented to the Court. Constitutional Issues Presented Regarding Discrimination:

  2. Whether the Seventh Amendment Right to a Jury Trial, in and of itself, protects a party against BIASED and CORRUPT JUDGES, as this Amendment to the Constitution was designed to do.

  3. Whether a “Partnership Agreement” created the morning of the first day of trial, in order to create a “Wrongful Withdrawal from a Partnership” definition, whereby the only way the withdrawal is not wrongful, is if the “undertaking is complete,” is UNCONSTITUTIONAL.

  4. Whether the Defendant’s creation of a “Wrongful Withdrawal from a Partnership” Definition that provides “No-Way-Out” for Plaintiff, “NoMatter-What” the Defendant did to her, i.e., Retaliated against Plaintiff because Plaintiff rejected her unwanted sexual advances, AS APPLIED, violates the Plaintiffs CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS.

  5. Whether the Appellate Court’s use of the standard “’REGARDLESS” OF WHAT PROMPTED THE SIGNING OF THE DEED, when the issue before the court was that the Defendant misrepresented to Co-Plaintiff what she was actually signing when the Defendant defrauded her into signing a deed.

  6. Whether an E-mail which states an Intention to send an E-mail to the Defendant’s family members, without evidence of the ACTUAL E-MAIL EVER BEING SENT OUT, is Defamation.

  7. Whether a Plaintiffs E-mail to Co-Plaintiff stating that the Defendant “stole $600” is Defamation because it is not true and based on the technicality that it is common knowledge that you can’t steal from a joint account for which you are a Co-Owner.

  8. Whether a Judge who has been put on notice by a Defendant who admits during testimony that the Plaintiff has “a history of psychological illness” can ignore a Plaintiffs (who alleges sexual harassment and retaliation) mental health status, as factors to consider regarding whether the Plaintiff wrongfully withdrew from a project and, more importantly, the Defendant’s retaliatory acts.

  9. Whether an Openly Gay Judge can legally protect a Gay Defendant who has knowingly committed OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE BY PERJURY and FRAUD UPON THE COURT because that Gay Defendant is a member of the same protected class.

  10. Whether an Openly Gay Judge’s disparity in treatment to the benefit of the Gay Defendant erodes the fair and balanced decision-making process that courts should strive to protect.

  11. Whether a court should recognize sexual harassment and retaliation when a perpetrator is a Gay Female, or whether it is a Heterosexual Males-Only Issue.

  12. Whether a finding of Systemic and/or Institutional Racism can be made in the Civil Court System when there is clear and convincing evidence that 14 White Lawyers committed Obstruction of Justice, Bribery, and Perjury against a Black Plaintiff, or whether such Racism is limited to Police Officers and the Criminal Court System only.

  13. Whether a Judge should be required to recuse herself when her platform, i.e., Championing the Rights of the LGBTQ Community, conflicts with her rulings 2& and there is clear and convincing evidence that she OBSTRUCTED JUSTICE in a court case that involves a Heterosexual Plaintiff versus a Gay Defendant, even if the direct issues do not involve whether a person is of a specific sexual orientation. Public Corruption Issues and Violations of Federal Laws Presented:

  14. Whether clear and convincing evidence of Judicial Misconduct and Public j Corruption, e.g., Bribery, Obstruction of Justice, Perjury of one party, should be grounds for an automatic appeal, without the other party being required to preserve error at the trial court level. i

  15. Whether an exception to the rule that a complaint about error at the trial ; court level must have been raised in the Court of Appeals before the Texas Supreme Court will review it, can be made when there is clear and convincing evidence of OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE, JUDICIAL MISCONDUCT, APPELLATE ATTORNEY MISCONDUCT AND OTHERj VIOLATIONS OF FEDERAL CRIMES EXIST.

  16. Whether there is a conflict of interest when an Appellate Attorney is representing an Appellant while he has a pending case in the same Judge’s court for which he is appealing that Judge’s decision.

  17. Whether a finding that a Judge has Obstructed Justice should be made when compelling evidence exists that her actions conceal the same issues coveredup in the Defendant’s Bribery attempt.

  18. Whether the Judge’s Reputation and the Public’s Sense of Trust in that Judge, although False, OUTWEIGHS the Public’s Need to Know the truth of the clear and convincing evidence of the Judge’s violations of Federal Law, including OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE, in favor of a Defendant. i

  19. Whether evidence of Pretexts for Discrimination, Judicial Misconduct and j Biases would include, a Judge who: l) allows new causes of action to be made the morning of the first day of trial, in violation of the Statute of Limitations and the Judge’s Own Scheduling Order, 2) allows new causes of action and Jury Charge questions to be formulated for one party the morning of trial, AFTER denying key witnesses on a timely-filed witness list for the other party, during a pre-trial hearing, 3) dismisses a credible, disinterested Bank witness from testifying without a known objection and a ruling on that objection, in order to protect that party, when it would expose that party’s PERJURY she committed, 4) allows a “Wrongful Withdrawal from a Partnership” Definition that, as applied, is UNCONSTITUTIONAL, 5) Obstructs Justice by carrying out the Defendant’s intent of the Bribe, by preventing Objections and Additional Questions to be added to the Jury Charge, because it would negatively affect the Defendant. :
