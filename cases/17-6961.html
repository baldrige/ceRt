Frank Dippolito v. United States, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit, No. 17-1048 · judgment August 10, 2017

Certiorari denied · January 8, 2018

Question presented

1). Did Congress exceed its constitutional authority when it legislated away inmates’ constitutional protections? 2). The Prison litigation Reform Act blocks inmates from the courts, allowing the Bureau of Prison to act inappropriately, with impunity, violating inmate civil rights. Encouraged knowing the Prison Litigation Reform Act prevents redress the PLRA keeps inmates from the courts and court remedies. Where can an inmate find redress and protection if not from the courts? Administrative Remedy Process is flawed, masterfully manipulated and controlled by BOP staff members (unit Teams). An inmate cannot file remedies successfully, BP-8 through BP-11 when the process is controlled by the administration from who redress is sought. Did Congress intend for inmates to have no solutions? What is the intention of Congress regarding the Prison Litigation Reform Act? The (SCA) Second Chance Act 42 USC 17541 section 231 chapter 1, provides for inmates Wseeking participation in the program, home confinement for 6 months and extended half way house for up to a year. The Second Chance Act was passed into law in 2007. The administration of section 231, of 42 USC 17541 chapter 1 improving Federal Offender re-entry program has been ignored. No real policy exists. Does the BOP have the power and legal right to ignore and circumvent US statue? Does the Attorney General, then Eric Holder, directed by statue (SCA) to oversee the implementation and administration of the Second Chance Act have the authority to ignore a directive mandating him to perform, statue 42 USC 17541 section 231? Does the Plaintiff have a property interest in the Second Chance Act? Do inmates have the same guaranteed protections as the general public from exposure to toxins / carcinogens and unsafe conditions? Can private for profit corporations as The American Correctional Association, who publish standards, legally give accreditations to institutions, for a fee, (tax payer dollars). When no effort or inspections are made to see the prison conditions equal their written standards? Do inmates have property interest in a non toxic living environment? Do inmates have property interest in protective winter clothing, when expected to remain outdoors all day every day? Does the Plaintiff have a constitutional right to be represented by appointed counsel, by protections 5th 6th 14th amendment, as the defendants are represented by appointed counsel in their of the and individual capacities by the US Attorney’s office?
