PennyMac Financial Services, Inc., et al. v. Richard Smigelski

Paid petition · Court of Appeal of California, Third Appellate District, No. C081958 · judgment December 19, 2018

Certiorari denied · October 7, 2019
Pre-decision estimate: 3% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

Whether the Federal Arbitration Act preempts a statelaw rule that prohibits the enforcement of an arbitration agreement in a dispute covered by that agreement unless the State consents, based on the fiction that the State is a party to the lawsuit, when in fact it is not.
