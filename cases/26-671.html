Questions presented

Petitioner prayed below 18 reliefs which were as Writs of Mandamus and/or Prohibition.

  1. Could this Court grant a Writ against United States that make amendment to the Constitution that Parental rights are Constitutional rights?

  2. Could this Court grant an Order that (i) US Govt/President should not appoint the US Supreme Court Justices and Chief Justice (ii) Thru Collegium process 51 USCA Judges should be appointed to US Supreme Court for 5 years, and they should retire at 70 whichever comes 1st. (iii) Most experienced USSC’s Associated Justice should be promoted to Chief Justice when Chief Justice retire or vacant (iv) Invalidate the Judge Brown appointment to US Supreme Court?

  3. Could this Court grant an Order that USCA Judges and All States Apex Court Judges/ Wash DC’ District of Columbia Court of Appeals should be appointed by US govt thru US Supreme Court’s Collegium process by inviting nationwide applicants?

  4. Could this Court grant an Order that (i) Circuit level collegium should select the US Dist Court /Bankruptcy/Magistrate Judges, Superior Court of the District of Columbia by inviting nationwide application and US Govt should appoint the US Dist Court/ Bankruptcy/Magistrate Judges . Washington DC Dist. Court Judges and (ii) Circuit level collegium should select the All States’ trial/Tax Court /Municipal Judges)and State govt should appoint these trial/Tax/Municipal Judges?

  5. Could this Court grant an Order to Union of India that US citizen kids should not be hold in India, and US citizen Kids need to return to US for their education, vacations, and holidays, parental rights and properly kids Ancestral inheritance property(s)/wealth need to transfer to the kids in USA?

  6. Could this Court grant the Petitioner’s prayer that this Court should strike down All States’ Constitution and States’ Supreme Court?

  7. Could this Court grant an Order that (i) Moving States’ Municipal Judges into States’ Judiciary payroll and (ii) States’ Municipal Mayor should not appoint any Municipal Judges and State Govt should not Appoint any States’ Judges including Municipal Judges (iii) Deposit traffic violations fine in States’ treasury, (iv) Remove the Petitioner’s traffic ticket to US District Court (from Woodbridge, NJ Municipal Court) (v) By party’s request Jury should be available in any trial Court including traffic ticket hearing/ municipal hearing?

  8. ould this Court grant an Order that States’ and it’s local Govt should not tow/taken away the home-less’s or less fortunate, marginalized people’s property(s)?

  9. Could this Court grant an Order that Strike down States’ NO LIABILITY INSURANCE COVERAGE ON MOTOR VEHICLE?

  10. Could this Court grant an Order to States that State should standardized the States’ Municipal Mayors and councilmen ‘s salary?

  11. Could this Court grant an Order that United States should enact United States Universal family Law?

  12. Could this Court grant an Order to United States to Abolish the Electoral College process of selecting President of the United States. The President and Vice-President of United States should be elected by House of Representatives?

  13. Could this Court grant an Order to United States for the changes in Article II of US Constitution, states with word “He” to “He/She”?

  14. Could this Court grant an Order to United States for the changes in “Twenty-Second Amendment’s Section 1 of US Constitution, states that US President can be elected only twice” to unlimited times US President/States’ Governor should be elected/re-elected?

  15. Could this Court grant an Order the each appeared defendant to pay $100 million for the Petitioner’s effort, pain and suffering, expenses, litigation cost or pain and suffering by litigation?

  16. Could this Court grant an Order that Elected official of United States (President, vice president, senators, House representatives) and their immediate family members should not buy or owe stocks/shares in Federal Contractors (including defense contractors)?

  17. Could this Court grant an Order the respondent Woodbridge Township should pay 295/day for TAKEN AWAY Porsche cayenne to the plaintiff?

  18. Could this Court order US Dept of States and other to issue my passport?
