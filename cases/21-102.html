Question presented

Whether attempted Hobbs Act robbery, in violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1951(a), is a “crime of violence” under 18 U.S.C. § 924(c)(1)(A), meaning that it “has as an element the use, attempted use, or threatened use of physical force against the person or property of another.” This Court granted review on this issue in United States v. Taylor, No. 20-1459, cert. granted July 2, 2021.
