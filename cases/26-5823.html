Patrick Dwayne Adams v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, No. 25-50063 · judgment May 19, 2026

Pending

Question presented

When a criminal defendant is not liable as a principal, can that defendant enter a knowing and voluntary plea of guilty to aiding and abetting a charged offense without first being informed that, to be convicted as an aider and abettor, the Government must prove that the defendant (i) had the requisite intent to further the entire and specific crime charged; and (ii) took an affirmative act to further at least one element of the charged offense?
