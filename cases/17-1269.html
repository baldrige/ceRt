Question presented

Whether a service packet is “addressed and dispatched * * * to the head of the ministry of foreign affairs” of a foreign state, as required by 28 U.S.C. 1608(a)(3), when the service packet is sent by registered mail to the head of the ministry of foreign affairs of the foreign state at the state’s embassy in the United States.
