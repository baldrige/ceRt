Sara Hutchinson v. Ed Putka

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Washington, Division 2, No. 58844-7-II · judgment April 29, 2025

Pending

Questions presented

  1. State Fair Housing Protection: Whether Washington courts erred in failing to interpret RCW 49.60.2235 - Washington's fair housing anti-coercion statute - consistently with its federal analogue, 42 U.S.C. § 3617, thereby undermining important housing rights and protections against coercion and retaliation for protected individuals (specifically disabled veterans and vulnerable persons) in real estate transactions.

  2. Procedural Due Process: Whether the trial and appellate courts violated Petitioner's right to procedural due process by excluding or disregarding material evidence and by reversing the trial court's initial summary judgment ruling (which had allowed Petitioner's discrimination claim to proceed) without any material change in circumstances - effectively depriving Petitioner of a fair opportunity to present her case to a jury.

  3. Equal Protection: Whether the decisions below deny Petitioner the equal protection of the laws guaranteed by the Fourteenth Amendment, where Petitioner - as an honorably discharged veteran with mental health disabilities - was deprived of the protections of state anti-discrimination law intended for her benefit, and whether the lower courts' handling of her claims failed to afford the careful consideration warranted for members of these protected classes.
