Willie Williams v. Douglas A. Collins, Secretary of Veterans Affairs

Questions presented

  1. Whether a veteran has a right to meaningful access to the courts and administrative agencies under the First Amendment of the Constitution when the government concealed court decisions subject to review under 38 U.S.C. § 7292(a) and (c), as provided in § 7292 of this title, and Subchapter II, § 7261, Scope of Review, concerning (a) any action brought under this chapter, and (b)(2) the requirement to take due account of the rule of prejudicial error; where the Judge disregarded material evidence, mandatory appeal procedures, statutory notice requirements, and the rule of prejudicial error in determining appellate juris­ diction?

  2. Whether it is a violation of this veteran’s First and Fifth Amendment rights when the Secretary and the Court resorted to an arbitrary [remand] classif­ ication contrary to 38 C.D.R. § 3.105 (revision of decision) and dismissed the appeal, prejudicially concluding that the appeal did not involve the validity or inter­ pretation of a statute or regulation, despite unresolved appeal-certification defects, conflicting judicial pre­ cedent, and the availability of extraordinary relief to protect potential appellate jurisdiction?

  3. Whether a veteran has a constitutional right to a fair hearing on the merits of his disability [remanded appeal], and whether this claim must be decided accord­ ing to fundamentally fair procedures, where determina­ tions have been subject to nothing but deferential review that continues with the same concealed evidence during adjudication, misstatements of material facts, misstatements of law, fraudulent submissions, and omissions?
