Trivansky Swington v. City of Waterloo, Iowa, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit, No. 17-1350 · judgment October 19, 2017

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

Attachment No. 1 I, enter the following questions of law based on Constitutional rights & Criminal Violations of State and Federal Law, and the questions listed below I wish for is court to decide upon: Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when B. Rollins made threats to the Plaintiff! Petitioner whom has numerous montal disabilities causing the plaintiff to feel unsafe and an overwhelming need to protect himself? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when Defendants Anthony Nai & Aaron Haas continued to push the Plaintiff up a flight of stairs which could of ended in serious injury of both parties as well as their conduct being against jail policy and State and Federal laws? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the Defendant Aaron Haas put the plaintiff in a choke hold or headlock and continued to cut off his air supply? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the Defendant Anthony Nai continued to punch the Plaintiff while he was already handcuffed and being restrained or detained by numerous officers? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the Defendant Aaron Haas elbowed the Plaintiff in the back of the head, shifted his weight to his elbow that he was pinning the plaintiffs head to the floor with then lifted himself up, push the plaintiffs head into the floor, and continued to knee the Plaintiff upper body and face while handcuffed and already being restrained! detained? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the Defendants; Julie Lein, Wyatt Landers, Todd Schmitt, B. Rollins, Anthony Nai, Zachary Holbach, Aaron Haas, and any other officer whom witnessed but fail to stop the brutality of the Plaintiff which is clearly Negligence, Assault & Battery, and Cruel and Unusual Punishment? Noting that all was knowledgeable of wrong doing. Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the Defendant Zachary Holbach dug his fingers into the tissue underneath the Plaintiffs Jaw-line and pulled which clearly could of resulted in fatal injuries? Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when the third shift officer responding to the medical emergency of the Plaintiff having a seizure stated "He does know he's not going to the hospital right"? Clearly denying his right to adequate Mendical Treatment. Was there not a violation of my Eighth Amendment Right per Constitution when Nursing! Medical Services failed to adequately treat the Plaintiff after the use of force, and numerous complaints by the Plaintiff through med kites and face to face encounters as well as asking that pictures are taken and injuries adequately documented. Anthony Thompson (Black Hawk County Sheriff) & Mark Herbst (Jail Captain) failed to hold the defendants accountable for excessive force, and failure to adequately treat the plaintiff after the use of force as well as the seizure. See; Hudson v. Mcmillian, 503 U.S. 1(1992), Estate of Davis by Ostenfeld v. Delo, 115 F.3' 1388 (81h Cir. 1997), Brown v. Lippard, 472 f. 2d 384 (5th Cir.2006). "Excessive Force". See Estelle v Gamble, 429U.S.97,103 (1976), Hill v Dekalb Reg'l Youth Det. Ctr., 40 F.3d 1176, 1187 (11th Cir. 1994), Estelle, 429 U.S. At 104; Jett v Penner, 439 F.3d 1091, 1096 (9th Cir. 2006), Gutierrez v. Peters, 111 F.3d 1364, 1369 (7th Cir. 1997) McRaven V.
