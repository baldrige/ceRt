Johnny Bedgood, Jr. v. Waste Management, Inc.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, No. 25-12372 · judgment June 22, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Whether, under 42 U.S.C. § 12112(b)(5)(A), an employee must make a new, specific request for additional accommodation when the employer already knows of the employee’s disability and need for accommodation and is participating in an ongoing medical-leave and retum-to-work process, or whether existing knowledge and contextual communications may suffice to trigger the employer’s continuing accommodation obligations.

  2. Whether Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 56 permits a court to treat an employer’s disputed account of medical-leave expiration, retum-to-work authorization, and attendance-related termination as established, despite contemporaneous medical and leave-administration records supporting contrary reasonable inferences, consistent with Tolan v. Cotton, 572 U.S. 650 (2014), and Reeves v. Sanderson Plumbing Products, Inc., 530 U.S. 133 (2000).

  3. Whether Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 26, 37, and 56 permit a district court to grant summary judgment on an independently dispositive employer-identity determination by relying on a corporate declaration first introduced after discovery closed, without adequately addressing disclosure compliance, the plaintiffs opportunity to challenge the declaration, and genuinely disputed evidence of corporate employment control, where the plaintiff contested that determination and the court of appeals affirmed on alternative grounds without expressly resolving the employer-identity issue.

  4. Whether the Fifth Amendment’s guarantee of a meaningful opportunity to be heard is implicated when a federal court of appeals affirms summary judgment based on disputed characterizations of medical-leave and retum-to-work records, and subsequently denies rehearing after the appellant specifically identifies overlooked material evidence and Rule 56 objections.
