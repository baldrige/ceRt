Micah Stubbs v. April Stubbs

Questions presented

  1. Whether a State may, consistent with the Four­ teenth Amendment’s Due Process Clause, require by local court rule that every application for pay­ ment of court-appointed counsel fees “shall be made ex parte,” with the application then resolved by default on the papers—after an opportunity for written opposition, but without any required case­ specific live hearing—and the resulting fee obliga­ tion effective upon entry and payable within 45 days.

  2. Whether a State denies procedural due process when, having authorized court-appointed counsel’s fees to be imposed through that mandatory ex parte procedure, its rules then extinguish the only mechanism for redetermining the obligation thirty days after appointed counsel withdraws—so that the post-deprivation evidentiary hearing the parent was promised can lapse, and here did lapse, with­ out ever being held, while every court holds review belongs somewhere else.
