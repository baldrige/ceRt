Questions presented

  1. Whether the Double Jeopardy Clause of the Fifth Amendment permits a defendant to seek habeas relief postjudgment but presentence pursuant to 28 U. S. C. § 2241 if the defendant can demonstrate a colorable claim that collateral estoppel principles of the Double Jeopardy Clause were violated during the defendant’s trial.

  2. Whether a judgment of conviction need not be entered, nor a sentence pronounced, where the conviction is void or voidable due to a violation of collateral estoppel principles of the Double Jeopardy Clause.
