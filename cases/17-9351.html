Desmond Otto Triplett v. Wyoming

IFP petition · Supreme Court of Wyoming, No. S-16-0298 · judgment December 15, 2017

Certiorari denied · October 1, 2018

Question presented

TO THE COURT The question(s) the Petitioner requests the Court to answer are: Trial Counsel: Did the State of Wyoming charge the petitioner with a crime that was never proven? Did defense counsel put the prosecution's case to the adversarial testing required by the Constitution? Was the defendant constructively denied counsel at both trial, and his "one and only direct appeal"? While investigating the allegations, did trial counsel's ineffective assistance and deficient performance prejudice defendant in his right to a fair and impartial trial? Did defense counsel refuse to present to the trial court that he had an actual conflict of interest? Did defense counsel refuse to present medical records that would show petitioner was physically incapable of performing the acts he is convicted for as his alleged accuser KW claimed he committed" Did defense counsel refuse to present medical evidence that would show functionally impotent due to physical damage? Did defense counsel refuse to ask for a sexual assault forensic evidence (SAFE) kit, ora physical evidence recovery kit (PERK),that may be used to exonerate the wrongly accused, and preserve the physical evidence following an allegation of sexual abuse from the victim which could have aided in the criminal investigation and the prosecution of a suspected assailant to be presented to support the alleged sexual abuse claim? Was DNA collected from petitioner prior to trial? Did defense counsel refuse DNA evidence to be used at petitioner's trial for his defense? DID defense counsel refuse to provide petitioner with a forensics expert witness that could use DNA evidence to identify any matching DNA of any individuals, (such as a perpetrators and victims), and also use his specialized methods (scientific, technical, or other), and extremely reliable technique's and opinion's about evidence, or about facts in blood, semen, skin, saliva or hair found at crime scenes before the court to educate the jury in DNA evidence that was collected to prove petitioners guilt or innocence? DID Defense Counsel allowed a jury pool often (10) women and two (2) men, and along with two (2) alternates that were women. This is a violation of Cross Section. Defense Counsel allowing this jury pool often (10) women and two (2) men, and two (2) alternates that were women and refusing to object to the biased nature of such a lopsided jury in light of the charge as he had lodged against him. Through this violation did Counsels errors prejudice the defendant and show a reasonable probability that the result of the proceeding would have been different? Did defense counsel refuse to enter cell phone and camera evidence that prosecution claimed to have contained illicit text messages and photos? Did defense counsel refuse to have prosecution demonstrate and justify where the illicit photos and text messages came from? Did defense counsel refuse to ask for voice recognition or speaker identification data through systems that can be used and have been trained on a specific person's voice to authenticate or verify the identity of a speaker? Did defense counsel refuse to make any objections during the trial and just merely let prosecution's case go unchallenged? Did defense counsel refuse to present a defense that is guaranteed by Constitution's, both (U.S., and Wyoming)? Did defense counsel refuse to call witnesses that were available to testifying in his behalf, thus violating petitioner's sixth amendment right? Did defense counsel refuse to contest that prosecutions false claim that petitioner purchased "sexy cloths" for the alleged victim? Did defense counsel refuse to object to prosecutions fraudulent claim of other victims that were not presented to the trial court or named so the petitioner could confront his alleged accusers that is guaranteed by the Constitution? Did defense counsel refuse to challenge the fact that the case was based upon hearsay testimony that was demonstrated to be inaccurate by the actual evidence available that prosecution failed to enter into the case? Did defense counsel refuse the fact that the charges changed three to five times in the pretrial phase and four to five times while witness testified on the stand that would show the witness was committing perjury as petitioner was actually innocent? Did defense counsel refuse to investigate the lies about the relationship with petitioners previous wife of how, when, and where their relationship began? Id. TTRC page 46 line 22-25 Did defense counsel refuse to present to the court discovery evidence of alleged allegations, found in sealed partial transcript dated September 15, 2015 by petitioner through due diligence upon receiving his discovery the 15 "' of January, 2016? Did defense counsel refuse to admit "extremely critical evidence" of the alleged victim's diary into evidence that clearly states how, when, and to whom she gave her virginity to that could show petitioners actual innocence? Did defense counsel also refuse other "extremely critical evidence" within the diary that clearly states alleged victim was sexually active with several other people and never once indicates sexual activity with the petitioner?
