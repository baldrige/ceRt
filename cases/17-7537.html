Ivan Speed v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 16-10056 · judgment October 24, 2017

Certiorari denied · February 26, 2018

Questions presented

  1. May an indigent defendant be forced to pay for the costs of mental health and drug testing and treatment as a condition of supervised release from prison?

  2. Is it error to impose a condition of supervised release prohibiting the defendant from drinking any alcohol where neither his crime nor his history involves alcohol abuse?

  3. Is a condition of supervised release requiring that the defendant shall not “frequent” gambling establishments vague or overbroad in violation of the Due Process Clause of the Fifth Amendment?

  4.
