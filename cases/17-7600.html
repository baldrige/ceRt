Benton G. Baskin v. Kansas

IFP petition · Court of Appeals of Kansas, No. 115,271 · judgment April 14, 2017

Certiorari denied · March 19, 2018

Question presented

Mr. Baskin alleged that the trial court erred when the chief trial judge granted trial counsel a continuance over the objection of his pro se motion pursuant to K.S.A. 22-3402(1) 2013 Supp., asserting his right to a speedy trial (4) days prior to the first jury trial setting on November 3, 2014, triggering the trial court's duty to inquire of the conflict between Mr. Baskin and trial counsel setting a trial date beyond the (90) day statutory time limit of the statute, Mr. Baskin was not present incoutt as required by K.S.A. 22-3208(7), and the Fourteenth Miendmentof the United States Constitution, chief trial judge failed to take judicial notice of the pro se motion asserting right to a speedy trial, December 15, 2014, trial counsel requested a continuance of the second jury trial setting, breaching the agreement to assert Mr. Baskin's right to a speedy trial, he was not present as required by Kansas Statute and the Fourteenth Amendment, nor was a written motion filed as required by K.S.A. 22-3402(g) 2012 Supp., January 26, 2015, the state filed a motion for continuance hearing for the conformity of K.S.A. 22-3402(a) 2014 Supp., Mr. Baskin's speedy trial right was violated as (116) days elapsed between arraignment on October 2, 2014 thru January 26, 2015, (26) days in excess of the statutory (90) day time limit of K.S.A. 22-3402(1) 2013 Supp., as a result of prosecutorial misconduct causing the delay, chief trial judge did not address Mr. Baskin on record to waive his right and withdraw his motion asserting his right to a speedy trial in open court, and further erred when he continued Mr. Baskin's jury trial to March 23, 2015 (56) additional days beyond the speedy trial time limit. Did the Kansas Court of Appeals err in deferring to the district court's findings when it failed to dismiss charges for violating Mr. Baskin's right to a speedy trial pursuant to K.S.A. 22-3402(1) 2013 Supp. ?
