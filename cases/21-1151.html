Questions presented

  1. Whether Respondent School District of Philadelphia, on August 25,1992, discharged appellant Arthur O. Armstrong, without just cause, without a hearing, without due process of law in violation of Article B-VIII -grievance Procedure of the Collective Bargaining Agreement without due process of law in violation of the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States.
