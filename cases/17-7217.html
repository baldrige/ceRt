Matthew Davis v. United States

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, No. 15-3578 · judgment April 18, 2017

Certiorari denied · February 20, 2018

Questions presented

  1. Whether the failure of the District Court after conviction but before pronouncement of sentence to inquire of petitioner consistent with 21 U.S.C. § 851(b) whether the petitioner affirmed or denied that he had been previously convicted as alleged in § 851, the Prior Conviction Information filed in this criminal action, and further inform him that any challenge to the prior conviction identified in the Information, may not be raised to attack his sentence, should result in the vacation of petitioner’s sentence.

  2. Whether the filing of a Prior Felony Information pursuant to § 851 approximately seventy days before trial and eight months after the original indictment and only after petitioner had made it clear he wished to exercise his constitutional right to go to trial demonstrated vindictive prosecution and entitles petitioner to re-sentencing or reduction of sentence to a mandatory ten years not the sentence as doubled by the § 851 filing.

  3. Whether the admission of the hearsay statements of the deceased alleged co-conspirator “Pudge” was error in that it was neither in furtherance of the conspiracy nor trustworthy. -i-
