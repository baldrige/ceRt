Juan Dalmau Ramirez v. Estado Libre Asociado de Puerto Rico

Paid petition · Supreme Court of Puerto Rico, No. MC-2024-0035 · judgment July 31, 2024

Certiorari denied · October 15, 2024
Pre-decision estimate: 4% cert probability

Before the decision, roughly the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for a petition filed soon after the judgment below.

Question presented

Does the ballot for the upcoming 2024 Plebiscite in Puerto Rico violate petitioner’s and the electorate’s federal constitutional rights to freedom of speech and association by not including a column to cast a vote for the “incorporation of Commonwealth” among the already-included options of Statehood, Independence and Free Association.
