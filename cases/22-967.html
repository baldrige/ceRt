Certiorari denied · May 15, 2023
Pre-decision estimate: 10% cert probability (95% interval 6%–15%)

Question presented

Did the Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit violate the Petitioner’s rights under the First Amendment when it sealed almost the entire transcript and almost all of the filings in a related habeas corpus proceeding? Do the Rules of the District Court for the District of Colorado unfairly and unconstitutionally restrict the ability and discretion of the trial court in ruling on a motion for sealing?
