Edward Shane West-El v. Mark J. Baker

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, No. 20-12126 · judgment January 21, 2021

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021

Questions presented

  1. In Earle v McVeigh, 91 US 503,23 L Ed 398, it says “ Every person is entitled to an opportunity to be heard in a court of law upon every question involving his rights or interests, before he is affected by any judicial decision on the question.” Why is the Petitioner not being heard as of yet?

  2. The Petitioner would like to know the reason why taxes were being deducted from his pay check, Secondly is the Petitioner getting the correct pay per hour as well as overtime, being the employee started work as a longshoremen in or around November 1996?
