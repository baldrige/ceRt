Erich Sorenson v. Massachusetts

Paid petition · Appeals Court of Massachusetts, No. 19-P-1170 · judgment November 16, 2020

Certiorari denied · October 4, 2021
Pre-decision estimate: 0% cert probability

Before the decision, well below the 4.1% base rate, with no standout signals pointing toward a grant.

Question presented

Like millions of other Americans, petitioner makes his home in an apartment building. The question presented is: Whether the hallway area immediately adjacent to an apartment, in a private multi-family dwelling that is not open to the public, is part of the curtilage of the home for Fourth Amendment purposes.
