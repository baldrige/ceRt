Jerry Alexander Menchu v. Multnomah County Health Department

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, No. 24-5902 · judgment May 13, 2026

Pending

Questions presented

The questions presented are:

  1. Whether an employee of a federally assisted program may sue under Tide VI, 42 U.S.C. § 2000d, for intentional discrimination in employment that falls within 42 U.S.C. § 2000d-3, or whether Tide VII displaces that claim — the Tide VI counterpart of the question on which this Court granted certiorari in Crowther v. Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, No. 25-183.

  2. Whether an employment practice falls within Tide Vi’s coverage under 42 U.S.C. § 2000d-3 where discrimination in that employment causes discrimination to the beneficiaries of a federally assisted program.

  3. Whether a plaintiffs ability to invoke Tide Vi’s private right of action presents a question of standing or one of statutory interpretation. See Lexmark International, Inc. v. Static Control Components, Inc., 572 U.S. 118,127-28 (2014).
