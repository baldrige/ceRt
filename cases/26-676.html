Questions presented

  1. Whether the Court should clarify its holding in Heck v. Humphrey, 512 U.S. 477 (1994), to apply the delay in accrual of claims under the Civil Rights Act to instances where the state courts ultimately do not strike down the state actions under review.

  2. Whether the Court of Appeals and the district court erroneously failed to apply delayed accrual under this Court’s holding in Heck v. Humphrey, 512 U.S. 477 (1994), as clarified, in finding that claims arising from a wrongful criminal conviction accrued, and limitations were not tolled, while the Plaintiff pursued his state judicial and executive remedies.
