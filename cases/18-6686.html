Grady Pederson v. Minnesota, et al.

IFP petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit, No. 18-1093 · judgment May 30, 2018

Certiorari denied · January 14, 2019

Question presented

Why was the State of Minnesota allowed to suppress evidence since 11/01/1995? Why were the Sheriffs' and Police Depts. Allowed to misrepresent facts, namely the Minnesota BCA report? Why was Judge Wietzema allowed to misrepresent the facts, imply guilt, namely the DNA report and the Minnesota Plea Agreement for 11/01/1995. Why did she not open up the file that shows that I am not a sex offender? Why were there civil commitments of Murray. Cottonwood and Nobles Counties? They are unfounded, unsubstantiated and they are a study of sex offenders, which I am not. Why am I made to register when the DNA evidence clears me of any association to any crime? Why is the state allowed to reach beyond the statutes of limitations, expired and served sentences? Why is the State of Minnesota allowed to cross lines of genocide? Why is the State of Minnesota allowed to exterminate a person from life when I am not guilty of any crimes they accuse me of? Why was the state allowed in the Counties of Murray, Cottonwood and Nobles to call me a sexual predator when I am cleared by DNA evidence? Why was the secondary charges of commitment and registry allowed to prosecute me? It is life, limb and double jeopardy. Respectfully Submitted:
