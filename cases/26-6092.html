In Re Michael McAfee

IFP petition

Pending

Questions presented

  1. Why are the Civil and Constitutional Rights of an innocent man constantly being violated? I have demonstrated time and time again that I’m not the perpetrator of the crime.

  2. Why was my rights violated when I tried firing my Public Defender, the Judge told me, "If I didn’t go to trial with Mr. Bruns, they would, have trial without me." I even asked Mr. Bruns for a change of Venue and he refused. I asked Judge Ellen Siwak to recuse herself and she said, "She would think about it." and the next day said, "She decided to stay on."

  3. Why did attorney David Bruns have evidence that belonged to me for a whole year and after he said, "He was ineffective for not turning it over?" The Judge said she would not allow him to say that he was ineffective.

  4. Why didn’t Mr. Bruns turn over exculpatory evidence to jury? In my evidentiary hearing, he said he didn't know why he didn't. He even admitted it could have gave reasonable doubt to my guilt.
