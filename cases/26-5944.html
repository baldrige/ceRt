Questions presented

  1. After amending Petitioner's original complaint, and without specifying anything new or different from the operative facts from the original pleading! Petitioner's question before the U.S. Supreme Court is, whether California abused it's discretion by not applying it's relation back doctrine to waive the three years statute of limitation provision?

  2. After the California Secretary of State obtained a dismissal to proceedings upon the original action, does Petitioner's change of party or naming the State of California on the amended complaint classify as 1). A new defendant - for purposes of serving an entirely new summons; or 2). A Substitution of Party - that would relate back to the filing and date of the original pleading?
