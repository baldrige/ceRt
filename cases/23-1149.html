Stephen M. Calk, aka Sealed Defendant 1 v. United States

Paid petition · United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit, No. 22-313 · judgment November 28, 2023

Certiorari denied · October 7, 2024
Pre-decision estimate: 12% cert probability (95% interval 8%–17%)

Before the decision, about 2.8× the 4.1% base rate. The model weighted this up for counsel with five or more prior petitions here, a Second Circuit decision below, and a circuit split argued in the petition.

Questions presented

  1. Whether something with no commercial or objective value can constitute a “thing of value” that “exceed[s] $1,000,” the receipt of which is punishable as a felony under the bank bribery statute, 18 U.S.C. § 215.

  2. Whether the bank bribery statute’s “corrupt” intent element requires a breach of a bank officer’s duty to act in the best interests of the bank or can instead be satisfied by acts undertaken with a good-faith belief they will benefit the bank.
