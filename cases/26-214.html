Questions presented

  1. Whether the Fourteenth Amendment permits a State to extinguish a non-titled spouse’s homestead interest through foreclosure without notice or a meaningful opportunity to be heard.

  2. Whether the Rooker-Feldman doctrine bars bankruptcy adjudication of a claimed homestead exemption and lien-avoidance motion when the state court did not decide those issues and the litigant lacked a meaningful opportunity to raise them there.

  3. Whether state courts may bar a litigant from filing pleadings in a manner that prevents adjudication of properly presented federal claims. /I ■I
