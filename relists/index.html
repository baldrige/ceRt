Relists counts the times the Justices considered a petition and put it off to a later conference; beneath it, the conferences it has been at. A redistribution after a “Rescheduled” entry, a call for a response or an invitation to the Solicitor General (CVSG) is mechanical and does not count, nor does a second distribution for the same conference. Status: Up next, relisted and set for a coming conference; Awaiting the SG, invited to file and not yet back on a conference list; Held, re-listed conference after conference while it apparently waits on a related case — shown as conferences since it was first considered, because that count measures waiting rather than reconsideration; No conference set, relisted before and not now scheduled. Grant forecast leads with the estimate that the petition is granted at any conference; beneath it, next is the estimate for its next scheduled conference and GVR the companion summary-disposition estimate. Paid petitions only. Estimates, not predictions about any case. A petition whose disposition wording the classifier does not recognise also reads as live; that is about 0.1% of a completed Term.
