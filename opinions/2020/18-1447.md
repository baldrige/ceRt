## Per Curiam

*Per Curiam.*

The judgment of the United States Court of Appeals for the D. C. Circuit is vacated, and the case is remanded for further proceedings consistent with the decision in *Federal Republic of Germany* v. *Philipp*, 592 U. S. 169 (2021).

*It is so ordered.*
