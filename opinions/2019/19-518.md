# Colorado Department of State v. Micheal Baca, et al.

No. 19-518 · Decided July 6, 2020

## Per Curiam

*Per Curiam*

PER CURIAM. The judgment of the United States Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit is reversed for the reasons stated in *Chiafalo* v. *Washington*, 591 U. S. 578 (2020).

*It is so ordered.*

JUSTICE SOTOMAYOR took no part in the decision of this case. JUSTICE THOMAS concurs in the judgment for the reasons stated in his separate opinion in *Chiafalo* v. *Washington*, 591 U. S. 578, 597–609 (2020).
