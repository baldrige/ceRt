# Sharp v. Murphy

No. 17-1107 · Decided July 9, 2020

> Prepared by Supreme Court Report from the PDF published by the Supreme Court of the United States. The PDF is the authoritative text; this copy can contain errors of conversion. Source: <https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/preliminaryprint/591US2PP_web.pdf#page=536>

## Syllabus

*Held*: The judgment of the Court of Appeals—which held that Congress has not disestablished the Creek Reservation and thus Mr. Murphy's crime is subject to exclusive federal jurisdiction because Mr. Murphy is an Indian and the crime occurred in Indian country—is affirmed for the reasons stated in *McGirt* v. *Oklahoma*, 591 U. S. 894 (2020).

875 F. 3d 896, affirmed.

## Per Curiam

*Per Curiam.*

The judgment of the United States Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit is affirmed for the reasons stated in *McGirt* v. *Oklahoma*, 591 U. S. 894 (2020).
