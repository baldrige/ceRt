    How this page is scored

    The forecasts

    Each paid petition gets two forecasts at each conference: the chance it is granted at that conference, and the chance it is ever granted. The scores here use the first. The second can be shown on the dot plot and is totalled in the table. Only paid petitions are scored.

    Written down in advance

    The forecasts come from the site's forecast ledger. The nightly run adds a petition the first time it appears on an upcoming conference's list and never edits the entry afterwards, and nothing is logged for a conference that has already happened. These are the numbers the site committed to, often weeks ahead. Each entry also carries a final forecast: re-scored every night while its conference is ahead and frozen at noon UTC on conference day, before the Justices meet, so it is the last forecast the site made with everything it knew. The switch at the top of the page scores either one. Both can differ from the conference pages, which re-score past conferences with the current model and the case as it stands now. An entry written by code later found to be broken is re-scored if its conference is still ahead, and left out if it is not; the ledger keeps the original numbers either way.

    The outcome

    Read from the orders released between the conference and the next one. Granted and GVR'd are as ordered. Carried over means the order lists named no disposition: relisted, held, rescheduled, a call for the Solicitor General's views, or only the IFP motion decided. Carried over and GVR'd both count as not granted at this conference. The granted-ever view uses everything ordered since the conference. Habeas, mandamus, prohibition and rehearing entries are left out.

    The yardstick

    The backtest figures are the conference model's rolling-origin results: trained on earlier Terms and scored on OT2019–OT2024, 11,716 petition-conferences with 346 grants. See the methods note. A Term's live record will be noisy for months: the Court grants a few dozen petitions a Term, and AUC and calibration from a handful of grants carry wide intervals.
