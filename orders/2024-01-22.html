Certiorari granted1

  1. 22-7466Richard Eugene Glossip v. OklahomaThe motion of petitioner for leave to proceed in forma pauperis and the petition for a writ of certiorari are granted. In addition to the questions presented, the parties are directed to brief and argue the following question: Whether the Oklahoma Court of Criminal Appeals' holding that the Oklahoma Post-Conviction Procedure Act precluded post-conviction relief is an adequate and independent state-law ground for the judgment. Justice Gorsuch took no part in the consideration or decision of this motion and this petition.
